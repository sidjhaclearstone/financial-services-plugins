## 3. What the market is doubting
At 13.1x the market is pricing in four doubts:
- (a) a dealer revolt against the Deal Builder rollout and package pricing, which cut forecourt numbers in FY26;
- (b) stock shortages from weak new-car supply in 2020–23;
- (c) agentic AI routing car searches away from the portal;
- (d) a UK small/mid-cap discount, with no governance or state issue.

Our view:
- (a) is partly right. Pricing will be held toward the low end of 5–7% and Deal Builder product terms were adjusted (FactSet SA, 21-May-2026).
- (b) is temporary.
- (c) is not visible yet. LLM-originated traffic is below 0.5% (FactSet SA, 21-May-2026), and Autotrader's real-time inventory and valuation data is an input agents need. The risk is genuine over five years, but Autotrader's audience lead is far larger than CarGurus has in the US.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY Mar-31 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = £0.499)
FY3 consensus of £0.497 already reaches the bar, so 2030–31 needs only ~0.2%/yr. From the FY Mar-26 actual of £0.341, the bar requires 6.6%/yr for five years. Buybacks give ~5% even at zero revenue growth.

Failure is about 18% after overlap, giving **P(Test 2) ≈ 80%**. FAIL. That is above CARG (75%) because the moat and margin are stronger and the margin is mid-history rather than at a peak. It is below TRU/INTU because of the FY21 collapse, recent ~8% delivery and cyclical UK dealer exposure.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY Mar-27 to Mar-31)
History: 7/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were FY20 (+5.5%), FY21 (−40%, COVID free-listing relief) and FY23 (+5.9%). The last three years were +8.5%, +7.8% and +7.6%, only just above the bar despite 2–3 pts of buybacks. Subscription revenue is ~80%+ (estimate), but dealers cut packages when stressed.
- FY1 (+12.6%, H1 reported in Nov-2026): P ≈ 0.85.
- Next 4 years: 0.6 × 0.75⁴ = 0.19 with no downturn, plus a downturn branch of 0.4 × 0.2 × 0.75³ = 0.03, for 0.22.
- 0.85 × 0.22 ≈ **18%**. FAIL. This is level with CARG/BRC.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether Autotrader can keep raising average revenue per retailer by ~5%/yr while dealer profitability is squeezed and AI agents mediate car search. Test 2 would move toward 85% if FY27 shows forecourts growing, ARPR ≥5% and FY27 EPS ≥ £0.384 without a pricing backlash. FX: there is negligible translation risk because revenue is almost entirely UK and in GBP. The USD market cap moves with GBP.
