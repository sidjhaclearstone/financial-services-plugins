## 3. What the market is doubting
Aon trades at 14.5x NTM with a 6.4% FCF yield, a discount to Marsh (16.8x; US analysis). The stock is down ~11% since June ($326 at the 9-Jun-2026 conference; StreetAccount). The doubts:
(1) The **P&C/reinsurance soft market** and rate cuts on fiduciary income.
(2) **NFP leverage** (2.3x) and integration.
(3) **AI disintermediation** of placement.
(4) A **weaker organic record than Marsh**: 6/10 years ≥7% EPS growth vs 9/10.
There is **no country, governance or state-ownership discount**. The Irish domicile is a tax structure, and the NYSE listing and US-style governance carry no discount.
On the evidence, (1) is real but partial: management stresses that nominal GDP correlates more with growth than pricing does (9-Jun-2026), and new business and retention are strong. (2) is fading: net debt fell from $16.6bn to $13.3bn in 2025 (FactSet). The discount looks mostly temporary.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
- FY1 2026: +8.7% consensus ($18.55 vs $18.26 needed; std $0.35, n=23; Q3 still to report) → P ≈ 0.80.
- History: 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2016 (+6.6%), 2017 (+2.3%), 2020 (+6.98%) and 2023 (+5.6%). EPS never fell.
- Revenue is largely recurring (renewal commissions and fees). Buybacks (~2.5 pts) cushion a weak year.
- Normal-year ≥7% ≈ 0.75 while the soft market persists.
- No-recession branch: 0.6 × 0.75⁴ = 0.19. Recession branch: 0.4 × 0.30 × 0.75³ = 0.05. Sum 0.24 × 0.80 = 0.19 → **P(Test 3) ≈ 18% → FAIL**. That is below MRSH (25%) because of the weaker record, and inside the ≤25% financials band.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether organic growth holds at ≥5% through a full soft-pricing cycle while buybacks restart at scale after NFP deleveraging. Sustained ≥6% organic with 50+ bp/yr margin expansion and $2bn+/yr buybacks would lift P(Test 2) to ~87%. Organic ≤3% or a new large leveraged deal would cut it to ~75%.
**FX:** EPS is in USD (reporting currency), but roughly half of revenue is outside the US (estimate). A stronger USD is a translation headwind; Aon partly hedges.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag:** the adj-vs-GAAP gap is 32.9% 5y avg, against only 0.3% latest (FactSet). The gap comes from the 2021 $1bn WTW termination fee (GAAP EPS $5.55 vs adj $12.00), recurring restructuring programmes, and NFP intangible amortisation. FY2025 GAAP ≈ adjusted, probably because one-off gains offset those charges (estimate). Restructuring has recurred often enough that part of it is a real cost. FCF conversion is 1.18x 10y / 1.12x 5y (FactSet), so cash backs adjusted earnings. This is a broker, so FCF is meaningful, though fiduciary balances add noise. **FCF check:** NTM 6.4% vs LTM 5.2% (2025 OCF − capex $3.22bn / $61.5bn).
