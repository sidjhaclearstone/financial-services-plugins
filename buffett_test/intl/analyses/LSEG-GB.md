## 3. What the market is doubting
At 16.3x with an 8% NTM FCF yield, the market is having the "terminal value debate", in the CEO's own words (FactSet transcript, 30-Jul-2026). The doubt is whether LLMs and AI aggregators commoditise the Workspace desktop and Data & Analytics, the largest division. Other weights are low headline ROIC after the Refinitiv deal, a GAAP EPS well below adjusted, and a mild UK-listing discount. There is no state-ownership or governance issue.

Our view is that the doubt is partly wrong. Proprietary data such as tick history, Lipper, FTSE Russell indices and LCH flows is the input AI needs. Long-term data agreements run up to seven years, and LSEG keeps the direct customer relationship even when it is accessed through MCP (FactSet SA, 26-Feb-2026). The desktop seat risk is real but affects a minority of revenue. FTSE Russell, LCH and Risk Intelligence are largely insulated.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2026–FY2030)
History: 7/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2016 (−1.7%), 2020 (+4.7%) and 2023 (−0.8%, when FX and Refinitiv investment hit). About 70% of revenue is subscription (estimate) and pricing is stable. Buyback support is strong (~4.5 pts), which carries a weak year.
- FY1 +14.5% is near-certain: P ≈ 0.95.
- P(no FX/market shock in the next 4 years) ≈ 0.55 × 0.83⁴ = 0.26. With a shock, P(still ≥7%) ≈ 0.25: 0.45 × 0.25 × 0.83³ = 0.06.
- 0.95 × 0.32 ≈ 0.30, cut to **28%** for GBP translation volatility. FAIL. This sits between TRU (25%) and INTU (30%).

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether AI agents make LSEG data more valuable through MCP licensing or turn the Workspace desktop into a commodity. Test 2 would move toward 88% on D&A organic growth accelerating to ≥6% with the margin step-up delivered and a normalised FY2026 capex near £1.1bn. FX: a ~10% GBP appreciation against USD/EUR would trim reported EPS ~5–6% (estimate).

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag.** The adjusted vs GAAP gap averages 135% over 5 years and is 76.5% on the latest figure (FactSet). The cause is amortisation of acquired Refinitiv intangibles, which is non-cash and from a past deal, so we accept it. FCF conversion vs GAAP NI is 2.01x over 10 years (FactSet). Against adjusted NI (£4.18 × 527m ≈ £2.20bn, computed), OCF − capex was 124% in 2024 but only 78% in 2025 because of the unexplained FY2025 capex spike. Watch FY2026 capex.
