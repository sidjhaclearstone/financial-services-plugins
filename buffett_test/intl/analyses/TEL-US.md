## 3. What the market is doubting
At 16x NTM the stock is cheap against FY2026–28 consensus growth of 14%/11%/yr. The market doubts three things. (a) FY2026 EPS of $11.45 may be a cyclical high, flattered by the hyperscaler capex boom ("DDN orders up 70% YoY", StreetAccount Q1-FY26 Q&A, 21-Jan-2026). (b) Auto production is flat at about 87–88m units (CEO, Q4-FY25 call), and Western OEMs are under pressure from Chinese exporters. (c) The margin is at a 10-year high. These doubts are partly right. TE is a cyclical component supplier, and EPS fell 23% in 2020. The doubt about durability is too strong, though. The structural drivers are electrification content, the $3bn AI target already surpassed (StreetAccount, 22-Jul-2026), and grid demand. There is no country or governance discount: TE is Irish-domiciled but US-listed, with a US shareholder base.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2027–FY2031)
- Recurring revenue is low: sales are design-in, but volumes are cyclical. Pricing power is moderate, mostly pass-through.
- Auto is about 55% of sales (Transportation segment, estimate), so volume sensitivity is high.
- In a weak year, buybacks add about 3 pts. That would not have offset 2020 (−23%) or 2023 (−8%).
- History: 7 of 10 years at ≥7%. Destocks occurred roughly every 3–4 years (2019, 2023).

P(no auto/industrial soft year in 5 years) ≈ 25%. P(each normal year ≥7% | no soft year) ≈ 0.9^5 ≈ 0.59. Product ≈ 15%, cut to **≈12%** because comps after the FY2026 +31% year are demanding. **FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether AI/energy (now a large share of Industrial/DDN) makes TE less cyclical. If TE held EPS growth through an auto down-year, with orders still above book-to-bill 1, P(Test2) would move to 85% or more. FX translation: TE reports in USD but earns about 2/3 of sales outside the US. A strong dollar would cut reported EPS by 2–4%.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adjusted-vs-GAAP gap averaged 4.2% over 5 years. FY2025 is an exception at 42% (GAAP $6.16 vs adjusted $8.76). The likely cause is a tax-related charge after a large FY2024 tax benefit (GAAP $10.33 > adjusted $7.56) (estimate). The 10-year FCF conversion of 1.01x uses FactSet FCF after dividends, so pre-dividend conversion is about 1.2–1.3x. Quality is good. Flag: one-year GAAP gap from tax items.
