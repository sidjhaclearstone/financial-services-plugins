## 3. What the market is doubting
At 15.9x NTM and a 6.9% FCF yield, a business with 25% EBIT margin and double-digit EPS growth is cheap. The doubts:
(1) **AI disruption.** AI site builders and agents (Lovable, Wix, hyperscalers) and AI search may reduce SMB need for classic websites and domains.
(2) **Control and governance discount.** United Internet holds the majority (around two-thirds per public disclosures; estimate, not verified here). Free float is small, related-party arrangements exist, and a squeeze-out or capital action could be on terms unfavourable to minorities.
(3) Hardware/memory cost inflation and capex.
(4) German small-cap/mid-cap de-rating.
On evidence, doubt (1) is real but not yet visible: net adds are at record levels and churn is low (Q1/Q2-26 calls). Doubt (2) is structural and probably right as a permanent discount. The low multiple is partly correct and partly a liquidity/control discount rather than an earnings doubt.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = €2.36)
FY3 consensus (€2.67, FactSet) is already 13% above the bar. The bar fails only if EPS falls >11% from the FY2028 level by FY2031, or if the consensus ramp does not happen (FY1→FY2031 needs only 5.4%/yr).

| Threat | Prob. (5y) | EPS impact | Contribution to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI erodes web-presence demand/ARPU (new customer volumes fall, price pressure) | 25% | −10 to −20% vs path | ~8% |
| Margin reversion from 25.7% peak (hardware costs, marketing, cloud capex) | 20% | −5 to −10% | ~5% |
| Large M&A misstep (management targets "larger technology" deals) | 10% | −5 to −10% | ~3% |
| SMB recession at the endpoint (churn, slower adds) | 15% | −5% | ~3% |
| Controlling-shareholder action against minorities (squeeze-out terms, related-party pricing) | 5% | variable | ~1% |

Σ ≈ 20%, less ~1 pt overlap → failure ≈ 19% → **P(Test 2) ≈ 80% → FAIL**. It sits just below GDDY (82%), which has the same AI threat. IONOS has a larger consensus cushion but a shorter record, majority control and less buyback support.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
- FY1 2026: +12.0% consensus (€1.82 vs €1.74 needed; std €0.17, n=12). Q3 is still to report and the year includes front-loaded marketing → P ≈ 0.80.
- Recurring revenue is >90% (hosting/domain subscriptions; estimate), and SMB churn is low. Recession sensitivity is low to moderate.
- History: 5/7 years ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2021 (−18.5%) and 2024 (−3.3%). These were GAAP items, IPO costs and marketing step-ups, not revenue declines, but they show the line is noisy.
- Normal-year ≥7% probability ≈ 0.80 for FY2027–28 (consensus +25%/+17%) and ≈ 0.70 for FY2029–30.
- 0.80 × 0.85 × 0.80 × 0.70 × 0.70 ≈ 0.27; × 0.80 for recession/one-off haircut → **~21% → P(Test 3) ≈ 20%, FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether AI products (AI Receptionist, agentic site-building) raise ARPU faster than AI-native tools erode new-customer demand. Two things would lift P(Test 2) to ~85%: net adds sustained ≥400k/yr with ARPU acceleration through 2027, and an EBIT margin held ≥25% while capex stays ~6–7% of sales. A fall in net adds or rising churn would cut it to ~70%. A United Internet squeeze-out at a low premium is the main minority risk.
**FX:** reporting is in EUR, mainly in Germany, UK, Spain, France and the US. A USD-based holder carries EUR/USD translation risk on the whole earnings stream.
