## 3. What the market is doubting
At 14x, against a long-run premium rating (estimate), the market doubts four things:
(a) **UK housing and RMI cyclicality.** EPS fell 29% in 2020 and 30% in 2023, and 2025 EPS is still 25% below the 2022 peak (FactSet).
(b) **Structural margin erosion.** EBIT margin is 14.9% vs a 16.4% 10y average (FactSet). Opex is growing with depot formats, digital and France.
(c) **A flat UK kitchen market** in 2026 (company planning assumption) and competition from Wren, Magnet and B&Q trade.
(d) **First sizeable acquisition** (DIY Kitchens, a direct-to-consumer business). Some investors read this as a sign the core model is maturing.

The doubt is **partly right**. The franchise is intact: Howden keeps gaining share and holds an industry-leading gross margin (H1-26). But EPS is genuinely cyclical, and pre-2020 organic EPS growth was only ~6%/yr.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 consensus is +4.2% (£0.510 vs a £0.524 threshold, std £0.009, high £0.530). P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0.07. Revenue is not recurring: it is transactional trade RMI. History: 5/10 years ≥7%, with three down years (FactSet). Buybacks (~1.5 pts) cannot offset a 20–30% RMI drawdown.
P ≈ 0.07 × 0.55^4 × 0.85 ≈ **0.5% → ~1%. FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether operating margin can recover toward 16–17% as UK volumes normalise, or whether ~15% is the new structural level. A UK RMI recovery in 2027 combined with DIY Kitchens accretion would push P(Test 2) toward 78%. A UK recession in 2027–28 would cut it to ~60%. FX: earnings are almost all in GBP, so a weaker pound lowers USD-translated EPS and FCF.

## 7. Earnings quality
No flag. Adj EPS equals GAAP (5y avg gap 0.0%, FactSet). FCF conversion is 1.02x 5y / 0.97x 10y (FactSet), but OCF is before ~£100m+ of IFRS 16 lease principal (estimate). On a lease-adjusted basis conversion is ~0.85–0.9x, which is still fine. Capex has stepped up (£157m in 2025 vs £61m in 2019) for manufacturing and depots.
