**FCF check (Phase 1), flagged by the reviewer: FAIL.**
- LTM FCF is depressed. FY2025 OCF was €368m and capex €279m, leaving €89m (FactSet). The capex includes the Milan Santa Giulia arena, which costs more than €400m gross (FactSet SA, 20-Aug-2026), and OCF was hit by ticket-float timing.
- The NTM 7.6% (~€450m, computed from FCFPS × 96m shares) needs two things: Milan capex rolling off ("largely complete"), and seasonal float inflows plus the LA28 project timing, which "benefited" H1-26 cash flow (FactSet SA, 20-Aug-2026). Float is promoters' and customers' money, not owner earnings.
- On a normalised basis, NTM net income is ~€346m (NTM EPS $4.04 ≈ €3.60 × 96m). D&A of ~€100m roughly equals maintenance capex of €80–100m (estimate). Minorities take a share of consolidated OCF. Owner FCF is therefore ≈ net income, a **5.5–6.0% yield**, which is the same as the 5.8% earnings yield implied by the 17.2x P/E.
- The ≥6% rests on float and capex timing, so it is not supported. The P/E route (17.2x > 15x) does not rescue it. **Test 1 FAIL**.

## 3. What the market is doubting
The P/E is 17.2x, not below 15x. Test 1 relied on the FCF route, which the check above rejects. The market doubts:
- (a) the quality of FCF, given float and venue capex;
- (b) the FY2025 EPS decline and the lumpy Live Entertainment segment, including festival cancellations;
- (c) the post-LA28 cliff in 2029;
- (d) antitrust exposure, with past Italian and German scrutiny of TicketOne and Eventim dominance;
- (e) a governance discount: KGaA with founder control, no buybacks, and new venue capital projects such as Milan.

Our view is that (a), (c) and (e) are real. (b) is cyclical. The core ticketing franchise is strong: management describes AI as deepening the moat and US competition in Europe as limited (FactSet SA, 28-May-2026).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2026–FY2030)
History: 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). Misses were 2016 (+4.6%), 2018 (+2.2%), 2020 (−162%) and 2025 (−14%). Revenue is transactional. In a weak year there is no buyback support. FY2 and FY3 consensus is only ~9%, and 2029 faces the LA28 comp.
- FY1: P ≈ 0.95.
- Next 4 years: 0.55 × 0.6⁴ = 0.07, plus a downturn branch of 0.45 × 0.1 × 0.6³ = 0.01, for 0.08.
- 0.95 × 0.08 ≈ **7%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether ticketing can compound at 6–8% with rising margins after LA28, while capital is returned rather than tied up in venues. Test 2 would rise toward 80% on FY2026 OCF − capex of ≥€400m without float effects, a buyback policy, and the November 2026 CMD targets confirming high-single-digit organic ticketing growth. FX: reporting is in EUR with mostly European revenue, so translation risk is low. LA28 brings some USD exposure.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adjusted vs GAAP gap is not flagged at 0.3% (FactSet). **FCF flag:** the reported FCF conversion of 1.47x over 10 years (FactSet) is flattered by ticket-float growth. OCF − capex over 2022–25 was €1.19bn against net income of €1.08bn (computed), about 110%, aided by float. FY2025 was 32% because of Milan capex. Net cash should not be read as fully excess, because it includes customer and promoter money.
