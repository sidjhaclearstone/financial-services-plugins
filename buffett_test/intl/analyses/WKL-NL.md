## 3. What the market is doubting
At 13.8x, a 10y low for a name that has historically traded at 25–30x (qualitative), the market is pricing in AI substitution. The specific fears are LLM legal research, AI tax preparation, and clinical AI competitors to UpToDate such as OpenEvidence. Other doubts are slower buybacks after a heavier M&A year, leverage up to 2.0–2.5x, and a USD-translation drag on EUR EPS. A CEO transition is under way: Stacey Caywood is designated CEO (FactSet transcript, 25-Feb-2026). There is no state-ownership or country discount; Dutch governance is shareholder-friendly.

Our view is that the doubt is overdone but not wrong. WKL's moat is integration into professional workflow and regulatory liability, not raw content. UpToDate Expert AI was expected to reach ~70% of enterprise customers by mid-2026 (FactSet SA, 25-Feb-2026), and recurring revenue still grows at 7%. A risk the market may be underweighting is that AI lowers the cost of building competing content.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2026–FY2030)
History: 8/10 years ≥7%, including 2020 (+7.9%) (FactSet). The two misses, 2018 (+5.6%) and 2025 (+6.4%), were both USD-translation years. EPS has never declined. Revenue is 85% recurring, so recession sensitivity is low. Buybacks add ~3 pts.
- FY1 (2026) consensus is +6.6%, with a mean of €5.641 against €5.66 needed (std 0.072, high €5.81): P ≈ 0.40.
- FY2027–30: 0.6 × 0.85⁴ = 0.31 with no FX shock, plus 0.4 × 0.3 × 0.85³ = 0.07 with one, for 0.39.
- 0.40 × 0.39 ≈ **15%**. FAIL. Excluding FY1, P would be ~38%, at the ADBE (37%) level. FY1 weakness breaks it, as it did for the US FY1 breakers.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether AI becomes a feature WKL monetises (Expert AI, agentic tax and legal) or a substitute that erodes retention and pricing in Health and Legal. Test 2 would move toward 88–90% on recurring organic growth of ≥7% through 2027 with stable retention, and on buybacks back above €1bn. FX: ~60% of revenue is USD-denominated (estimate), so each 10% USD fall cuts EUR EPS ~5–6%. This is the main source of single-year misses.
