## 3. What the market is doubting
Little. At 24.0x NTM EPS and ~3.4% on aggregate FCF consensus, Halma is priced as a quality compounder. There is no country, governance or state-ownership discount: it is a UK-listed, widely held FTSE 100 name. **The high stage-1 FCF yield is a data artefact.** The NTM FCFPS of $2.92 implies ~$1.11bn (~£0.82bn at the ~1.34 USD/GBP implied by the price). That is ~1.9x the aggregate FY Mar-27 FCF consensus of £440m (n=9) and ~1.55x NTM EPS. That cannot be reconciled with Halma's ~100% FCF conversion: 10y 1.04x (FactSet), no customer float, working-capital growth with 16% organic growth, and capex rising to £71m. The likely cause is contributor mix in the per-share FCF consensus (few brokers, possibly pre-tax or pre-capex "cash flow" definitions; estimate). Real yields:
- LTM (FY Mar-26): OCF − capex £383m / £12.9bn = 3.0%
- FY Mar-27 consensus: £440m → 3.4%
- FY Mar-28 consensus: £501m → 3.9% (FactSet, FMP)

The market's only real doubts are whether the photonics boom is cyclical and whether M&A returns hold at higher deal prices. ROIC has eased from ~14–15% to 11–13% (FactSet).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY Mar-27 to Mar-31)
- FY1 FY Mar-27: +17.7% consensus (std £0.030, n=16; £1.22 needed) → P ≈ 0.97.
- History: 9/10 years ≥7%; the only miss was FY Mar-21 (+2.2%, COVID). EPS never fell (FactSet). Recurring revenue is moderate: consumables, service and regulation-mandated replacement (estimate). Buybacks give no support.
- Normal-year ≥7% ≈ 0.85.
- No-recession branch: 0.6 × 0.85⁴ = 0.31. Recession branch: 0.4 × 0.25 × 0.85³ = 0.06. Sum 0.37 × 0.97 = 0.36, × 0.9 for the photonics comparison risk in FY2–FY3 → **≈ 33% → FAIL**. That is at the upper end of the compounder band, level with COR (33%) and below BR (40%).

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is how much of the FY Mar-26/27 acceleration (organic +16%, guided low double-digit) is a durable photonics franchise versus a cyclical AI-capex spike. If photonics is durable and non-photonics organic growth holds ≥6%, P(Test 2) rises to ~87%. A photonics bust in FY Mar-28/29 would cut P(Test 3) to ~15%. The valuation (24x, ~3.4% FCF yield) fails Test 1 regardless.
**FX:** EPS is in GBP, with a large USD revenue share (US is the largest market; estimate ~40%+). A stronger GBP is a translation headwind.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag:** the adj-vs-GAAP gap is 15.6% 5y avg and 16.2% latest (FactSet), just over the 15% line. It comes from amortisation of acquired intangibles and acquisition costs, which are a real and recurring cost of the M&A model. FCF conversion is 1.04x 10y / 1.05x 5y (FactSet), so cash backs reported earnings. **FCF check: FAIL.** The 6.4% stage-1 yield rests on an NTM FCFPS consensus that is inconsistent with aggregate FCF consensus and history. The true NTM FCF yield is ~3.4%. With P/E at 24.0x, Test 1 fails on both routes.
