## 3. What the market is doubting
WTW trades at 14x, below MRSH (16.8x). On 30-Apr-2026 the stock fell to ~$249 (−14% on the day) after Q1 organic growth of +3% (R&B +2%) and investor fear of AI disruption of broking and HWC consulting (StreetAccount). The doubts:
(a) **AI.** It could commoditise HWC (actuarial, benefits administration, career consulting) and placement.
(b) **Soft P&C market** and falling fiduciary income.
(c) **Margin at a 10y peak**, with FY3 consensus depending on Propel.
(d) **Organic growth trails peers**, and the company has a habit of portfolio reshuffles.

Our view is that the doubt is **partly wrong**. Organic growth rebounded to +5%/+7% R&B in Q2-26. Management argues AI lowers cost to serve while accountability and carrier negotiation still need a broker (Q1-26 call). Buybacks alone deliver ~3.5 pts. The **right** part of the doubt: HWC (~45% of revenue, estimate) has more AI exposure than broking, and Career saw a ~50% drop in Middle East project work (Q2-26 call).

Σ ≈ 18%, less ~1 pt of overlap → failure ≈ 17% → **P(Test 2) ≈ 83%. FAIL.** This is just below MRSH (85%). WTW has an easier consensus path, but a weaker franchise, more AI-exposed consulting and a less consistent history.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 2026 is +15.9% vs an 18.28 threshold (low estimate $19.21), so P ≈ 0.98. History: 6/10 years ≥7%. Two of the four misses were divestiture years (2021 −0.9%, 2025 +0.9%) and two were near-misses (2017 +6.9%, 2020 +6.8%) (FactSet). Revenue is ~70%+ recurring (renewals, retainer and outsourcing; estimate), and buybacks add ~3.5 pts in a weak year.
- Normal-year pass rates FY27–30: 0.80, 0.78, 0.70, 0.70 → product 0.31
- Recession branch (40% within 5y, cuts that year's pass rate to ~0.45): factor 0.6 + 0.4 × 0.6 = 0.84
- 0.98 × 0.31 × 0.84 ≈ 0.25; less ~3 pts for the habit of dilutive disposals, and capped at peer AON (18%), which has the stronger record → **P(Test 3) ≈ 18%. FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether Propel and AI lift the margin to ~30% by 2028 without losing HWC revenue. If organic growth holds at ≥5% through 2027 with visible savings, P(Test 2) moves to ~88%. If HWC organic turns negative because of AI substitution, it falls to ~70%. FX: WTW reports in USD, but about half of revenue is non-US (estimate); a stronger dollar is a translation headwind (FY26 FX tailwind guided at +$0.35, StreetAccount 30-Apr-2026).
