## 3. What the market is doubting
HDFC Bank has historically commanded a premium P/E of 20x+ (estimate). At 12.3x NTM the market doubts four things:
(a) **Post-merger growth/funding.** The loan-to-deposit ratio is above system, loan growth has been sub-system, and NIM has been compressed. Brokers (HSBC, JPM, Kotak; StreetAccount 20-Apr-2026) prefer ICICI on growth and core earnings.
(b) **Governance and management.** There was an ethics/governance controversy in March 2026: management told an investor meeting on 18-Mar-2026 that "there is nothing wrong from an ethical perspective". A review reportedly found no major shortfalls (Reuters via StreetAccount, 6-May-2026). An external CEO, Anup Bagchi, was then appointed (StreetAccount 5-Oct-2026).
(c) **The RBI rate-cut cycle** compressing margins.
(d) **Country/flow discount.** Foreign selling of Indian financials and INR weakness.

Our view is that the doubt is **mostly temporary**. ROA held at ~1.9% through the merger digestion, asset quality is stable, and deposit growth is improving. The **right** part is that the pre-merger 18–20% EPS compounding will not return; ~12% is realistic. State ownership is not an issue: the bank is privately owned and widely held.

FY3 (FY29) consensus ₹71.35 is already above target. At only 8%/yr from FY27 EPS would reach ~₹77.6. What would make FY32 EPS lower?

Σ ≈ 18%, less ~2 pts of overlap → failure ≈ 16%. Applying the bank/cyclical calibration (US banks 65–78%; cyclical → usually <80%) with India's structural cushion gives **P(Test 2) ≈ 82%. FAIL.** It sits above CBSH (78%) because the required 5.4%/yr is less than half its normal-year stack.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY27–FY31)
FY1 (FY27) consensus is +8.4%: ₹52.83 vs a ₹52.16 threshold, std ₹1.14, z ≈ 0.6, low estimate ₹49.40. P(FY27 ≥7%) ≈ 0.65 after allowing for typical estimate drift at Indian banks. History: 9/10 adjusted years ≥7% (merger FY24 the exception), and every pre-merger year including COVID passed. But GAAP EPS growth was <7% in 2 of the last 3 years. Bank earnings depend on credit costs, and there are no buybacks to support EPS.
- Normal-year pass rate 0.85 → 0.85^4 = 0.52 for FY28–31
- Credit-cycle branch (30% within 5y, that year's pass rate ~0.4): factor 0.7 + 0.3 × 0.47 ≈ 0.84
- 0.65 × 0.52 × 0.84 ≈ 0.28. Applying the bank calibration ("banks rarely >25%"), the merger-era misses and the rate-cut NIM headwind → **P(Test 3) ≈ 22%. FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether ROA can rise from ~1.9% back toward pre-merger levels of ~2.0–2.1% (estimate) under a new CEO while loan growth runs above system with a falling LDR. Two quarters of NIM expansion plus ≥15% loan growth would move P(Test 2) to ~86%. Evidence of a broader governance problem or an Indian unsecured-credit cycle would cut it to ~70%. FX: EPS and growth are in INR, which has historically depreciated ~3%/yr vs USD (estimate). USD EPS growth runs correspondingly lower, and the P/E is quoted in USD at today's rate.

## 7. Earnings quality
No flag. Adj vs GAAP gap is −3.1% 5y avg (FactSet). **FCF conversion is not meaningful** for a bank. The net effect on FY26 EPS is small (estimate), but the FY26 base is slightly flattered.
