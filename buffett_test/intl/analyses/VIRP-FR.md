## 3. What the market is doubting
At 14.9x, roughly in line with a de-rated animal-health sector (US peer ZTS is at 12.5x in the US run), the market doubts:
(a) **US companion-animal slowdown** (fewer vet visits) and parasiticide pressure.
(b) **Heavy capex.** The 2026 cash-generation guide is only ~€80m after ~€125m capex, and 5y FCF conversion is 0.61x (FactSet).
(c) **Growth reliant on M&A**, with execution memories of 2015–17, when the FDA problems at the St Louis plant cut EPS 65% in 2017.
(d) **Family control and low liquidity.**

The doubt is **partly right**. H1-26 revenue +7.4% and adj EBIT +7% are healthy, but H1 net income was only +6% (StreetAccount 17-Sep-2026), and EPS growth was +0.2% in 2023 and +2.8% in 2025. The 13% FY1 consensus needs a strong H2.

Σ ≈ 20.5%, less ~1.5 pts of overlap → failure ≈ 19–22% → **P(Test 2) ≈ 78%. FAIL.** That is above ZTS (70%, which carries specific Librela and competition issues), but capped by Virbac's operational history and its capex/FX exposure.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 consensus is +13.3% vs a €19.54 threshold (low estimate €20.01, z ≈ 2.4). Because H1 net income was only +6%, we take **P ≈ 0.80** rather than ~0.99. History: 7/10 years ≥7%, but **2 of the last 3 missed** (2023 +0.2%, 2025 +2.8%), plus 2017 −65%. Animal health is fairly recession-resistant, but there are no buybacks to support EPS.
P ≈ 0.80 × 0.65^4 (normal-year pass) × 0.85 (recession factor) ≈ **0.12 → P(Test 3) ≈ 12%. FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether the industrial transformation is delivered on time and on spec, and whether margin can rise from ~17% toward 20% once capex peaks. Two years of ≥10% EPS growth with FCF recovering to >€150m would lift P(Test 2) toward 85%. Another plant or regulatory incident would cut it below 60%. FX: EPS is in EUR, and most sales are in non-euro currencies (USD, INR, AUD, CLP, JPY, estimate). EM currency weakness is a recurring translation drag; a USD investor also carries EUR/USD exposure.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag: FCF conversion 0.61x 5y** (0.83x 10y) (FactSet). The causes are a capex step-up (€102m in 2025 vs €23m in 2019), inventory build, and Sasaeah/Mopsan acquisition cash in 2024–25. Management expects capex to stay above €100m for a few years (StreetAccount 18-Sep-2026). Adj vs GAAP gap is small (1.2% 5y avg).
