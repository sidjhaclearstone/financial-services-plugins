## 3. What the market is doubting
At 17x NTM, below its historic 25–30x (estimate), the market doubts three things:
- **(a) AI/LLM search could disintermediate listing portals.** This is the dominant fear for all classifieds.
- **(b) Competition.** A competitor is pricing aggressively, though management sees "no material market share loss" (StreetAccount Q1-26 Q&A).
- **(c) Spain integration risk** and the move away from a pure-German story.

The doubt is partly wrong. Scout24 is turning AI into revenue (60% of customers use ImmoPoints; "exponential growth in AI usage", Q2-26 call). Its proprietary listing supply and valuation data are what an LLM would need to license. No country or governance discount applies, apart from the general European internet de-rating.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2027–FY2031)
- Recurring revenue is high: about 75–80% subscription/membership (estimate).
- Pricing power is demonstrated.
- Volume sensitivity is low: EPS kept growing through the 2022–23 transaction collapse.
- Buybacks add about 3–4 pts in a weak year.
- History: 8 of 9 years at ≥7%; the one miss was a disposal artefact.
- Risks: deal-driven distortions (Spain), a re-investment year for AI, a regulatory change, and the AI-traffic shock.

P(no shock year) ≈ 0.50 × P(normal years ≥7%) ≈ 0.95^5 ≈ 0.77 → ≈38%, cut to **≈30%** for M&A and AI noise. **FAIL.** This is in line with INTU (30%) and GDDY (28%).

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether AI interfaces route property seekers around ImmoScout24 or through it as a paid data/inventory layer. Evidence of LLM licensing revenue, together with stable traffic share through 2027, would move P(Test2) to 88% or more. FX translation: Scout24 reports in EUR with Germany/Austria/Spain revenue. A USD investor carries EUR/USD exposure only, with no operating translation.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adjusted-vs-GAAP gap averaged 21.4% over 5 years, which is **>15%: flag**. Adjusted EPS excludes PPA amortisation of acquired intangibles (the 2014 KKR/H&F carve-out and later deals), share-based pay and M&A costs. The latest gap is only 4.3%, as PPA rolls off. FCF conversion of 1.16x over 10 years (after dividends in FactSet) is healthy.
