## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x / FCF yield 7.1%)
(a) **COVID "pull-forward" of deaths.** Industry volumes stay soft: Q1-26 −6%, FY26 guided −1 to −3% (StreetAccount 30-Apr-2026), and management sees flat volumes until the baby-boomer inflection after 2029 (StreetAccount 6-May-2026). EPS has gone nowhere since 2021.
(b) **Cremation mix and low-cost entrants** lower revenue per case.
(c) **Leverage and earnings sensitivity.** 3.8x ND/EBITDA, plus trust-fund income ($300–350m, StreetAccount) and preneed sales that depend on equity markets.
(d) **Accounting changes.** The shift to insurance-funded preneed alters revenue recognition.

Our view is that (a) is **temporary**: management says the COVID overhang is "fully absorbed" (StreetAccount 13-May-2026), and the demographic wave is close to certain. (b) is **partly right** and caps price/mix. (c) is real but bounded, because demand is non-cyclical. The market is also discounting how long it must wait for growth from the 2030s demographic driver.

Σ ≈ 20%, less ~3 pts overlap → failure ≈ 17%. We round down to **P(Test 2) ≈ 80%** given thin coverage and the stack's +0.5 pt cushion. FAIL. Demand is about as non-cyclical as it gets, but growth leans on leverage-funded buybacks.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 is +8.8% (threshold $4.12 vs mean $4.19, std $0.022; Q3 and Q4 still to report; guide $4.05–4.35, StreetAccount 13-May-2026): P ≈ 0.85. History: 6/10 years ≥7% (misses 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024; FactSet). The miss years were driven by volumes, which are not recession-driven. Each 1% of volume is ~$0.10 of EPS (~2.4%; StreetAccount 13-May-2026), so a mild flu season can push a year below 7%. Normal-year P ≈ 0.62.
- 0.85 × 0.62^4 ≈ 0.13; × ~0.75 for market/recession risk to preneed and trust income ≈ 0.10
- **P(Test 3) ≈ 9%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is when funeral volumes inflect (demographics) relative to cremation mix pressure on revenue per case. Two years of positive comparable funeral volumes with ≥8% EPS growth, and leverage held below 4x, would lift P(Test 2) to ~85%. Continued volume declines into 2027 would cut it to ~70%.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adj-vs-GAAP gap is 0.8% 5y avg (FactSet), so no flag. FCF conversion is 0.90x 10y / 0.88x 5y (FactSet), above 80%. Reported FCF ($484m FY2025) is held down by growth and cemetery-development capex ($458m, FactSet). Management's ~$700m FCF figure is on its own definition. Preneed accounting (deferred revenue, trust income) makes reported EPS sensitive to market returns.
