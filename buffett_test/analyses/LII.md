## 3. What the market is doubting
The shares are at $355.78 (FactSet close 19-Oct-2026), down roughly 32% from about $522 in late April 2026 (StreetAccount, 29-Apr-2026). The market doubts three things. (1) Residential volumes: Q4-25 residential volumes fell 32% y/y on channel destocking after the R-454B pre-buy, new construction is weak, and analysts saw more destocking ahead (StreetAccount, 28-Jan-2026). (2) Guidance: the original 2026 EPS guide was $23.50–25.00 (company, Jan-26). Consensus is now $23.39, below the low end of that range, so 2026 is roughly flat. (3) Peak margin: the 20.3% EBIT margin compares with a 14.5% 10-year average and was helped by refrigerant-transition pricing. Low-tier residential pricing is getting more competitive (Barclays conference, Feb-26). My view: the volume doubt is mostly **temporary**, because destocking ends and the installed base keeps ageing. The margin doubt is **partly right**: some normalisation from 20% is plausible, though Lennox's direct-to-dealer model and $75m of cost actions (2026 guidance) mitigate it.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × 23.39 = $30.41)
Consensus FY3 is $27.87 (2028). Growing that at 4.4%/yr over 2029–31 reaches $31.7, so the base case passes. What would leave EPS below $30.41 in five years?
- (a) Margin mean reversion to ≤16–17%, sustained: P 15%. Revenue of ~$7.1bn (5%/yr, estimate) × 17% gives EBIT ~$1.2bn. With 12% fewer shares, EPS is ~$27–29, which fails.
- (b) A housing/HVAC downturn in 2030–31 coinciding with the measurement year: P 10%, EPS -15–25%.
- (c) Structural pricing erosion or share loss (Asian ductless/heat-pump entrants, normalised refrigerant pricing): P 6%.
- (d) Capital allocation misstep, larger leveraged M&A or tariff shock: P 3%.
Σ = 34%, less ~6 pts overlap between (a) and (b) = **28% failure → P(Test2) ≈ 72%**. FAIL (< 90%). The cyclical, peak-margin exposure keeps it below 80%, consistent with the calibration anchors.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 (2026) consensus is +1.0%, and even the high estimate ($23.83) is only +2.9%. With about 10 weeks of the year left and a std of $0.22, P(FY1 ≥ 7%) ≈ 1–2%. History: 8/10 years ≥7%, but 2020 was -11.2% and 2025 +2.6% (FactSet). Recurring revenue is a replacement-parts/service mix rather than contracted revenue, and volumes are visibly cyclical. Even ignoring FY1, P(no recession hit in 4 yrs) ~0.6 × P(normal year ≥7%)^4 ~0.8^4 ≈ 0.25. Including FY1: **P(Test3) ≈ 3%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether the 20% EBIT margin is structural or the product of the SEER2/R-454B pricing cycles. Evidence that would raise P(Test2) toward 85%: HCS margins holding ≥19% through the 2026 volume trough, plus a residential volume recovery in 2027 without price give-backs. Evidence that would lower it: price competition spreading from the low tier, or BCS/HCS margins slipping below 17%. At about 14x trough-ish EPS, this is a classic "track for dislocation" name.
