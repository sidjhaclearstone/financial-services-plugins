## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x)
RBC (Apr-2026) noted the NTM P/E is "at 10yr lows". The shares fell 8.84 on the Q4-25 print and again on Q1-26 (StreetAccount). The market doubts five things:
- **Libre growth is slowing.** Q1-26 CGM grew +7.4% against a much higher historical CAGR, citing OUS tender delays and tough comparisons, with competition building (Stifel, BTIG).
- **Nutrition volumes are weak** after a strategic price reset (Q4-25 themes).
- **Diagnostics still faces headwinds.** COVID roll-off and China VBP were about a $1bn headwind in 2025 (CEO, Jan-2026).
- **Exact Sciences dilutes in year one.** It costs about $0.20 of EPS in 2026 plus early financing costs (Stifel). It also adds exposure to blood-based colorectal screening competitors (estimate).
- **NEC infant-formula litigation** is unresolved.

Assessment: **mostly temporary.** The COVID and VBP drags have been lapped, FX helped, EP is accelerating with two new PFA catheters, and FY1 EPS of $5.52 is a new high above the 2022 peak. The doubt is **partly right** on two points. CGM is becoming a more competitive, more price-sensitive category, and the Exact deal takes away the buyback lever for 2–3 years.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
The bar is 1.30 × $5.52 = **$7.17**. FY3 (2028) consensus of $6.73 (low $6.48) leaves +6.5% to find over FY2029–31, about 2.1%/yr. An 8%/yr path from FY3 gives $8.48, an 18% cushion.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of next 5 years)
- History: **6/10** years at ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2016 +2.3%, 2022 +2.5%, 2023 −16.9% and 2024 +5.2%. The COVID testing swing caused part of this, but it shows the EPS line is not smooth.
- FY1 (2026, one quarter left) at +7.1% sits right on the line (bar $5.51, mean $5.52, std 0.027) ⇒ P ≈ 0.55.
- FY2 at +10% (low +6.6%) ⇒ 0.75. FY3–FY5 at ~0.72 each: thin buybacks, and FX, respiratory season and launch timing can each cause a 6% year.
- In a recession, healthcare demand holds up. Haircut ×0.85 for FX, litigation and integration.
- P ≈ 0.55 × 0.75 × 0.72³ × 0.85 ≈ **13%** ⇒ **FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The main question is whether Libre returns to double-digit growth and Exact Sciences delivers high-teens growth with accretion from 2027. If both happen, ABT compounds at ~10% with low volatility. CGM growth recovering to ≥15% plus net debt back below 1x by 2028 would lift Test 2 toward 87%. A second year of CGM deceleration or a large NEC loss would push it toward 75%.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag (ADJ_GAAP_GAP).** The adjusted vs GAAP gap averages 20.9% over 5y and was 38.5% in FY2025 (FactSet). It comes mainly from amortisation of St Jude and Alere intangibles (~$2bn/yr, estimate) plus restructuring and litigation items. Exact will add more amortisation. GAAP 2024 ($7.64) was inflated by a tax benefit. FCF conversion on CFO−capex was 0.82 in FY2025 ($7.4bn ÷ ~$9.0bn adjusted net income). FactSet's fcf_conversion (0.89 5y / 1.11 10y) uses FF_FREE_CF, which nets off dividends, so it is not like-for-like. Underlying conversion is acceptable.
