Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation** — a true oligopoly/monopoly (sole US builder of nuclear carriers, one of two nuclear-sub yards) with secular Navy demand. But it has a monopsony customer, ROIC down to 7.8% and five years of execution misses, so management/capital-allocation excellence is not evident.

## 3. What the market is doubting
The shares fell from ~$371 (Feb-2026) to $269.64 (FactSet SA headlines; FactSet close 19-Oct-2026) despite a $76.6bn submarine award (~$25bn to Newport News) and a supportive budget. The market doubts that margins will ever get back to 9–10%. Shipbuilding margins have been flat to down through 2025–26 and guidance has been missed repeatedly (EPS −18% in 2024). FY2024 FCF was ~$26m (FactSet). Verdict: **partly right, partly temporary**. The demand side is not in question; execution is. The labour, hiring and throughput data are improving (FactSet SA, Jul-2026), but the margin step-up is back-end loaded to 2027+.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $24.24)
That is below the FY3 (2028) consensus of $24.61. Even with no margin recovery (6% revenue growth + 2% buybacks, pension/tax −5%), FY2031 EPS ≈ $18.65 × 1.06⁵ × 1.02⁵ × 0.95 ≈ $26 (estimate). So failure needs margins to regress again or large charges.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | Fail contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Margins regress below 5% / new contract charges (CVN, Columbia, LPD) | 30% | −15–25% | 14% |
| Budget/CR disruption or program deferral | 10% | −5–10% | 5% |
| Labour/supply-chain shock or large one-off loss | 10% | −10–20% | 6% |
| Pension income fade / tax normalisation larger than expected | 15% | −3–5% | 3% |

Survival = 0.86 × 0.95 × 0.94 × 0.97 = 0.75 → **P(Test2) ≈ 75% — FAIL**. History argues against generosity: EPS has gone nowhere since 2018.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% every year FY1–FY5)
History: only 5/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), with swings of −30% to +63%. Defense demand is recession-insensitive, but fixed-price execution and pension accounting make the EPS line lumpy. FY1 +21% (P 0.97), FY2 +13.4% (P 0.65), FY3 +16.4% (P 0.6), FY4–5 (P 0.55 each): 0.97 × 0.65 × 0.6 × 0.55² ≈ **10% — FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether shipbuilding margins actually step up to 8%+ in 2027–28 as COVID-era contracts roll off. Two consecutive quarters at ≥7% shipbuilding margin with FCF at guidance would raise P(Test2) to ~85%. Another charge would take it to ~60%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted vs GAAP gap 0% (FactSet): no flag. FCF conversion 0.82x (5y) / 0.85x (10y) (FactSet): passes, but it is lumpy (FY2024 FCF ~$26m). Watch that a meaningful share of EPS is non-cash pension (FAS/CAS) income (estimate). That is lower-quality earnings.
