## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x NTM / FCF yield 6.2%)
MRSH has historically traded at a premium P/E in the low-to-mid 20s (estimate, not sourced in our data). At 16.8x with a 6.2% NTM FCF yield, the market is pricing in a slowdown. The doubts:
(a) **The soft market.** P&C and reinsurance rates are falling, and R&I organic growth slowed to +3% in H1 2026 (StreetAccount).
(b) **Peak margin.** The 25.3% EBIT margin is at the 100th percentile of the last 10 years (FactSet).
(c) **AI disintermediation fears** for the broker and consulting model.
(d) **Falling fiduciary interest income** as rates come down, plus higher leverage after McGriff (2.4x ND/EBITDA).

Our view is that the doubt is **mostly temporary or wrong**. Brokers earned EPS growth through the 2015–18 soft market (+11–15%/yr, FactSet). Group organic growth actually improved to +5% in Q2 2026. Management says AI is changing staffing mix rather than demand (StreetAccount 15-Oct-2026). The **right** part of the doubt is that pricing-driven organic growth of 7–9% in 2021–23 will not repeat. This is a high-single-digit compounder, not a double-digit one.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $13.61)
FY3 consensus is $12.57 (FactSet), so 2029–31 needs only ~2.7%/yr. What would make 2031 EPS lower?

| Threat | Prob. (5y) | EPS impact | Contribution to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recession in 2030–31 (Consulting and transaction-risk lines hit) | 15% | −5 to −8% | ~4% |
| Prolonged soft market plus rate cuts, organic ~2% | 15% | growth ~4%/yr → fails ~1/3 | ~5% |
| AI/direct-placement or commission-regulation pressure on brokerage | 7% | −5 to −10% | ~3% |
| Large deal misstep, E&O/litigation, leverage | 5% | −5% | ~2% |

Σ ≈ 14%, less ~1 pt overlap → failure ≈ 13% → **P(Test 2) ≈ 87%**. FAIL, near-miss. It sits just above ADBE/CPAY/TRU (85%) because it has no single disruption threat and EPS still grew through 2020. It stays under 90% because about half of R&I growth is tied to the insurance pricing cycle.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 2026 consensus is +7.4% ($10.47 vs a $10.43 threshold, std $0.068; Q4 still to report). P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0.75. History: 9/10 years ≥7%. The only miss was 2020 (+6.7%), so even the COVID year nearly passed (FactSet). Revenue is largely recurring (renewal commissions and fees), but Consulting and pricing are cyclical. Buybacks (~1.5 pts) help in a weak year.
- No-recession branch: 0.6 × 0.82^4 = 0.27
- Recession branch: 0.4 × 0.30 × 0.82^3 = 0.07
- × FY1 0.75 → 0.75 × 0.34 ≈ **0.25 → P(Test 3) ≈ 25%**. FAIL. That is the top of what a quality compounder can reach while the soft market caps organic growth.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is how much R&I organic growth survives a full softening cycle. If organic growth holds at ≥4% with 30–50 bp/yr of margin expansion through 2027, P(Test 2) moves to ~90%. If it falls to ≤2%, or margins fall from the 25% peak, P(Test 2) drops to ~75–80%.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adj-vs-GAAP gap is 8.7% 5y avg (FactSet) and **15.8% latest**. FY2025 is just over the 15% line because of amortisation of acquired intangibles (McGriff) and integration and restructuring charges. This is a mild flag. FCF conversion is 1.09x 10y / 1.04x 5y (FactSet), so cash backs the earnings. Fiduciary interest income is a rate-sensitive component of earnings.
