## 3. What the market is doubting — why 16x (and a 6% FCF yield)
The shares fell from ~$198 (Feb-2026) to $157 before the Q4 print (SA headlines, FactSet) and now trade at $175, 16.1x NTM. Historically they traded well above 20x. The doubts are: (a) the 2026 "AI eats software/services" de-rating of fintech and data names; (b) fear that tokenisation or direct/pass-through voting disintermediates the proxy plumbing; (c) cyclical exposure of event-driven revenue ($250–300m guided FY27) and trade volumes to a market downturn; (d) an FCF yield inflated by working-capital gains (FY26 FCF +17%, CFO, 4-Aug-2026).
Our view: (a) is largely **wrong**. Broadridge's moat is regulatory and network-based (it is the beneficial-owner communications agent for nearly all US brokers), and mgmt reports 20%+ AI productivity gains in managed services (SA, 2-Mar-2026). (b) is a **real but slow** risk; Broadridge is positioning itself as the tokenisation rail (digital asset servicing rollout). (c) is **temporary**: FY2020 and FY2023 still grew ≥7%. (d) is **partly right**. Normalised FCF yield is closer to ~5.5% (estimate), still attractive.

Of the peers in this screen, BR has the most recurring and least cyclical EPS line (10/10 years ≥7%). It still lands just under 90% because of the regulatory and disintermediation tail, which is consistent with ADBE/CPAY/TRU at 85%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2027–FY2031)
History: 10/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), including FY2020 (+7.9%) and FY2023 (+8.5%) under a rate shock. About 75% of revenue is recurring (estimate; fee-based, multi-year contracts). Weak years are cushioned by buybacks (a $1.5bn authorisation, SA 4-Aug-2026). The problem is the small cushion: normal growth is ~9–10%, and the FY27 guide floor is 8%.
- FY2027: consensus +9.9%, std $0.08 → P(≥7%) ≈ 0.90
- P(each later normal year ≥7%) ≈ 0.85; P(market recession/bear year in FY28–31) ≈ 0.45, P(≥7% that year) ≈ 0.55 (FY2020 passed with only 0.9 pts to spare)
- 0.90 × 0.85³ × (0.55×0.85 + 0.45×0.55) = 0.90 × 0.614 × 0.715 = 0.40
- **P(Test 3) ≈ 40%**. FAIL. This is the top of the compounder band, alongside ADBE (37%).

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether proxy/communications economics survive tokenisation and pass-through voting. If Broadridge becomes the record-keeping and communications agent for tokenised securities, the moat widens and P(Test 2) would move above 90%. A negative change would be an SEC/NYSE fee-schedule review, or recurring revenue growth falling below 5% for two years.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag (moderate).** The adj vs GAAP gap is 25.3% 5y avg (FactSet), above the 15% threshold, mainly from amortisation of acquired intangibles (Itiviti, FY2021) and restructuring. The FY2026 gap shows as 0% in FactSet, where GAAP diluted EPS equals adjusted at $9.60. That may reflect a one-off gain or a data artefact, so we treat it as unverified. FCF conversion is 1.12x 10y / 1.14x 5y vs GAAP NI (FactSet), and mgmt cites 110% of adjusted earnings in FY26 (CFO, 4-Aug-2026), helped by working capital. Capitalised client conversion costs are a minor watch item.
