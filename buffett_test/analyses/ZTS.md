## 3. What the market is doubting
The shares fell from ~$121 (Feb-2026) to $79.59 (FactSet SA headlines; FactSet close 19-Oct-2026) on two guidance cuts. Behind them: (1) branded competition in dermatology and parasiticides, where Zoetis had very high share; (2) a declining US pet-care market, with fewer vet visits and owner price sensitivity; (3) Librela safety-related decline (−26% y/y in Q3-25, FactSet transcript). Mgmt calls much of it cyclical and sees no generic threat in core categories for several years (FactSet SA, May/Sep-2026). Verdict: **partly right**. Competitive share loss in derm is structural to some degree (new entrants still being adopted). The market slowdown is more likely temporary. A franchise with 23% ROIC at 12.5x is pricing in more permanent damage than the evidence supports.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $8.08)
EPS needs 5.4%/yr from a depressed FY1 base, or 5.35%/yr after the FY3 consensus of $6.91.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | Fail contribution (net of overlap) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural share loss in derm/parasiticides (Apoquel, Cytopoint, Simparica Trio) | 30% | growth ~2–3%/yr | 20% |
| US companion-animal demand stays weak (vet visits, affordability) | 20% | −1–2 pts/yr | 5% (overlaps above) |
| Librela/pain franchise does not recover | 15% | −2–3% EPS | 5% |
| Pipeline (CKD, long-acting Cytopoint) disappoints | 15% | −1 pt/yr | 3% |

Survival = 0.80 × 0.95 × 0.95 × 0.97 = 0.70 → **P(Test2) ≈ 70% — FAIL**. Buybacks (~4 pts/yr) and the cost program give a floor, but they cannot reach 5.4% without some revenue growth.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% every year FY1–FY5)
FY1 consensus is −3.1%, guidance was reaffirmed in Sep-2026 and three quarters are reported, so FY1 ≥7% is ~1% likely. History was 8/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), including 2020 (+5.8% — actually <7%; the misses were 2020 and 2022). **P(Test3) ≈ 1% — FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether derm/parasiticide share loss stabilises in 2027 and the CKD launch lands. A return to ≥5% organic growth in 2027 with stable derm share would lift P(Test2) to ~85%. Another guide-down in Feb-2027 would push it toward 55–60%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted vs GAAP gap 6.4% FY2025 (5y avg 7.6%, FactSet): no flag; it is mostly amortisation of acquired intangibles. FCF conversion 0.79x (5y) / 0.86x (10y) (FactSet): **mild flag** at just under 80%, explained by elevated manufacturing capex ($586–732m in 2022–23, FactSet). Note that the 2025 buybacks ($3.2bn) were partly debt-funded.
