- **A compelling customer value proposition**: engineered, durable identification and safety labels, with a razor/razor-blade printer-plus-consumables model.

## 2. Forward driver stack (normal year, post-PSS)
Honeywell PSS is an all-cash purchase for $1.4bn. PSS (mobile computers, scanners, printers) had ~$1.1bn of 2025 sales, so the deal roughly doubles revenue. Funding is cash plus new debt, taking net debt to ~2.5x EBITDA, and management targets <2.0x within two years. The deal is guided at ~$0.80 of year-one adjusted EPS accretion and $25m of cost synergies by year three (FactSet news Apr-26 and transcripts May/Sep-26). Close was targeted for 1-Aug-2026.

## 3. What the market is doubting
Brady's share price was $95.76 in Feb-26 and is $81.82 now. It trades at 12.2x NTM, below its own history, for three reasons:
- **Deal risk.** PSS is a hardware business Honeywell chose to sell, it competes with a stronger leader in mobile computing, and its growth had stalled.
- **Leverage.** The deal swaps a net-cash balance sheet for ~2.5x net debt/EBITDA.
- **Weak US manufacturing and European auto exposure** (FactSet news Feb-26).

Assessment: the doubt is **partly right**. The legacy business is a steady compounder: EPS grew ≥7% in 9 of the last 10 years. The acquired business brings lower margins, product-cycle risk and integration risk. The doubt is temporary only if synergies land and PSS stabilises.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in EVERY one of the next 5 years)
- **History:** EPS grew ≥7% in 9 of the last 10 years. The miss was FY2020 (−5.7%), so the history is good but not recession-proof.
- **Recurring revenue:** the consumables share falls once PSS hardware, which is more cyclical, is added.
- **Arithmetic:**
  - P(FY2027 ≥7%) ≈ 0.9 (deal accretion).
  - P(FY2028 ≥7%) ≈ 0.7 (consensus +10.2%).
  - P(FY2029–31 each ≥7%) ≈ 0.65³ ≈ 0.27, with a recession risk inside the window.

Overall ≈ 0.9 × 0.7 × 0.27 ≈ 17%, plus correlation credit, gives **≈18% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether PSS can be stabilised and returned to growth under Brady while leverage falls below 2x. If it shrinks, the deal accretion fades and the leverage limits buybacks.
- **Upgrade:** PSS organic growth turning positive in FY2028, leverage below 2x by FY2028, and synergies above the $25m target.
- **Downgrade:** PSS revenue down mid-single digits, or a goodwill impairment.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag: ADJ_GAAP_GAP.** Adjusted EPS was 23% above GAAP in FY2026, and the 5-year average gap is 11.2% (FactSet). The add-backs are acquired-intangible amortisation and deal costs, and the gap will widen after PSS. FCF conversion is 0.99 over 10 years but 0.86 over 5 years (FactSet), which is acceptable. FY2022 had a working-capital build (FCF $75m).
