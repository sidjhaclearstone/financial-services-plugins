# GoDaddy, Inc. (GDDY-US) — Buffett driver analysis
As-of 2026-10-19 | NTM P/E 12.2 (FactSet, 19-Oct-2026) | Mkt cap $13.5bn (FMP, 19-Oct-2026; price $106.70 FactSet close) | RBICS Software and Consulting / Internet and Data Services / Internet Support Services | Confidence: Medium
Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation**. Two of the four attributes apply. The first is a compelling customer value proposition: the largest domain registrar, with sticky renewals and a one-stop small-business stack. The second is exceptional capital allocation: about 100% of FCF goes to buybacks. The moat is not clearly widening, because AI site-builders and agentic discovery could erode the A&C upsell layer.

## 3. What the market is doubting
The stock is down from $135.72 (Aug-2025) to $106.70 (FactSet/StreetAccount). The doubt is AI disruption: LLM-built websites and agentic commerce could bypass the GoDaddy funnel and slow A&C bookings. A&C bookings decelerated to high-single-digit, and analysts were sceptical of reacceleration (FactSet StreetAccount, Aug-2025; transcript Q2-2026). Core platform bookings are only mid-single-digit. The doubt is **partly right** for the upsell layer and **probably wrong** for the core. Domain share is stable, mgmt has launched LLM-facing APIs, and Airo has a $10m run-rate (FactSet transcripts, Apr/Jul-2026). A domain remains the identity layer for any AI-built site. At ~8.5x FCF the market is implying ex-growth.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Bar: 1.30 × $7.40 = **$9.62** (FactSet consensus FY1 2026). FY2 consensus is $9.12 and FY3 $10.22. Buybacks alone at ~6–8%/yr reduce the share count ~30% over 5 years, so EPS would roughly clear the bar even with flat net income.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | P(causes fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI erodes website/hosting/A&C attach; new-domain volumes fall | 25% | −10–20% vs path | 10% |
| Renewal-pricing backlash / ICANN or registry cost pass-through failure | 10% | −5–10% | 3% |
| Capital-allocation change (large dilutive M&A, buybacks halted) | 15% | −5–15% | 3% |
| GAAP one-offs (tax, impairment) depress FY2031 EPS | 10% | variable | 3% |

Survival = 0.90 × 0.97 × 0.97 × 0.97 = 0.82. Demand is recurring and non-cyclical, and pricing power is demonstrated. However, one credible 5-yr disruption threat exists. **P ≈ 82% → FAIL** (calibration: good business with one credible threat, 70–85%).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +7% in every one of the next 5 years)
- Recurring revenue is high: domains and hosting renew, often prepaid multi-year. Bookings and FCF grew through 2020 (FCF $636m → $698m; FactSet).
- Recession sensitivity is low to moderate: SMB formation slows, but churn is low. Buybacks of ~8% give a floor in a weak year.
- History: 3/7 measurable years ≥7% (FactSet actuals). The misses are mostly GAAP noise (2018, 2020 impairment/tax; 2024–25 tax normalisation after the FY2023 benefit) rather than operating declines, since EBIT rose every year.

P(each year ≥7%) ≈ 0.80, so 0.80^5 = 0.33. Haircut ×0.85 for GAAP one-off risk gives **~28% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether AI reduces new-domain and website demand or monetises through it (Airo). Two signals would lift Test 2 to ~88%: A&C bookings reaccelerating to double-digit, and new-customer growth from Airo. Domain registrations declining year-on-year would cut it to ~70%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adj ≈ GAAP (5-yr gap 0.4%; FactSet). FCF conversion is 1.40x (5y) and 2.15x (10y) (FactSet). FCF is flattered by prepaid multi-year deferred revenue and SBC add-backs, so SBC is a real cost that offsets part of the buyback. GAAP EPS history is distorted by tax items, notably FY2023. No adverse flag; flag SBC and tax noise.
