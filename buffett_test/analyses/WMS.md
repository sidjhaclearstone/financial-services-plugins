## 3. What the market is doubting (why the FCF yield is ≥6% and P/E ~18.6x)
WMS fails the P/E route by a hair (18.6x) and qualifies on a 6.1% NTM FCF yield. The FCF yield is genuine: FCF/net income is 1.32x over 10y (FactSet), helped by D&A (~$216m) running above maintenance capex and by GAAP amortisation of acquired intangibles. The market is doubting four things:
- **Peak margins.** EBIT margin is 22.7% against a 15.4% 10y average (FactSet). EPS has gone nowhere for four years.
- **Resin and freight.** Management says resin cost inflation peaks in Q2–Q3 of FY27, freight stays "significantly elevated", and the sequential EBITDA-margin decline from Q1 to Q2 will be worse than the usual ~300bp (Aug-2026).
- **Construction exposure.** Residential and agricultural demand is soft. Only non-residential is growing (mid-single digit).
- **Integration and leverage.** The ~$1bn NDS deal lifted net debt/EBITDA to 1.8x, and management says it prefers M&A to buybacks.

The shares fell from $149.38 (6-Aug-2026) to $127.78. Assessment: **mostly temporary on resin, partly right on margin.** Price/cost discipline held through the FY2023–25 resin swings (margin stayed at 22.7% or higher), and conversion is a real secular driver. But FY2023's 25% margin probably included price/cost catch-up, so through-cycle EPS growth looks more like ~8% than the consensus 18–20%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of the next 5 years)
- FY1 (FYE Mar-2027, Q1 reported): consensus is −0.4%. The high estimate ($6.80, +8.5%) is the only one above $6.71. **P(FY1 ≥ 7%) ≈ 3%.**
- History: 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), including −32% in FY2019, −7.8% in FY2024 and +5.1% in FY2023.
- Demand is construction-linked and non-recurring, and resin pass-through lags.
- **P(Test3) ≈ 1% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether the ~23% EBIT margin is a structural level (mix, recycled resin, pricing discipline in a consolidated HDPE pipe market) or a resin-cycle high. Two signals would lift Test 2 toward ~82%: margin holding ≥22% through the FY27 resin spike, and NDS delivering the FY28 consensus step-up. A housing downturn that coincides with resin inflation would push it toward 65%.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adj vs GAAP gap is 10.6% on a 5y average, but **15.2% in FY2025 (flag, just over 15%)**: adj $6.27 vs GAAP $5.44 (FactSet). The causes are amortisation of acquired intangibles (Infiltrator, Orenco, NDS), deal/integration costs and stock-based compensation. FCF conversion is strong: 1.32x over 10y and 1.01x over 5y (FactSet). Watch for capex rising to ~$200m+ (FY27 guidance) and for whether NDS earns its cost of capital. ROIC fell from 23.2% (FY2022) to 12.6% (FactSet).
