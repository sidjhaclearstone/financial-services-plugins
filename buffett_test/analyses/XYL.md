## 3. What the market is doubting (why it trades below 18x)
XYL used to trade at a premium (RBC noted an 11% premium to peers in Oct-2025, when the stock was $152). It is now 17.0x NTM at $103.53, after falling to $111.85 (May-2026) and $124.97 (Jul-2026). The market is doubting four things:
- **Organic growth.** Total orders fell in Q3-25 on weaker WSS/WI capital projects. UBS downgraded the stock in Apr-2026 (target $132) on poor visibility in Measurement & Control Solutions after Badger Meter's Q1 miss. Consensus sales growth is only +1.8% for 2026.
- **The end of policy tailwinds.** Federal (IIJA) stimulus is sunsetting, ESG fund flows are falling (Mizuho, Oct-2025), and China remains weak.
- **Margin dependence.** EBIT margin is at a 10y high (14.6%, 100th percentile), and EPS growth now relies on 80/20 margin gains rather than volume.
- **Return on capital.** ROIC is ~7% after Evoqua.

Assessment: **mostly a de-rating from a premium multiple, and partly right.** EPS consensus still calls for ~10%/yr with very tight dispersion (FY1 std $0.05), the balance sheet is unlevered, and municipal water demand is non-discretionary. The doubt about the top line is fair: if 80/20 margin gains slow before volume re-accelerates, growth falls to ~5–6%.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
The bar is 1.30 × $5.649 = **$7.34**. FY3 consensus is $6.79, so FY3→FY6 needs only ~2.6%/yr. From FY1, it needs 5.4%/yr.

Arithmetic: 8 + 9 + 6 + 3 = 26%, less ~4 pts of overlap (the margin and metering threats are correlated) ≈ 22% fail. **P(Test2) ≈ 78% → FAIL.** This is in line with ATMU (78%) and below GEHC (80%). The balance sheet is strong, but industrial cyclicality (2020) and a growth path that depends on margin keep it under 80%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of the next 5 years)
- FY1 (2026, three quarters reported): consensus is +11.2%, and the low estimate ($5.52) is +8.7%. **P(FY1 ≥ 7%) ≈ 0.97.**
- History: 8/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2019 (+4.9%) and 2020 (−31.8%), so the recession year failed badly.
- About half of revenue comes from utilities and is fairly resilient. Industrial, dewatering and capital projects are cyclical, and buybacks are too small to bridge a weak year.
- Arithmetic: 0.97 × P(no industrial/municipal downturn in FY2027–30) 0.55 × P(each normal year ≥7%) 0.8⁴ = 0.41 → 0.22, plus ~0.01 for growing ≥7% through a mild downturn → **≈ 22% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether organic volume re-accelerates (data centres, metering replacement, reuse) before the 80/20 margin runway is used up. Two things would lift Test 2 toward ~85%: organic orders growing mid-single digit in 2027 with a 23%+ adj EBITDA margin delivered, together with larger buybacks. A slowdown in municipal capex after IIJA, coinciding with flat margins, would push it toward 70%.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag: the adjusted vs GAAP gap is 26.6% on a 5y average and 29.5% in FY2025** (adj $5.08 vs GAAP $3.92, FactSet). The causes are mainly amortisation of acquired intangibles (Evoqua, Sensus), plus restructuring and 80/20 simplification charges, which recur every year. FCF conversion is 1.11x over 10y and 0.96x over 5y against GAAP net income (FactSet). Against adjusted net income (~$1.24bn), FY2025 FCF of $873m converts only ~0.70x. ROIC of 7.3% (FactSet) is below a reasonable cost of capital on the acquired asset base.
