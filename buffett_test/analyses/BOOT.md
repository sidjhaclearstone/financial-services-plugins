## 3. What the market is doubting
Shares are down from $183 (Feb-2026) to $119 (FactSet/StreetAccount price tags) as comps slowed from +11.7% (Jul-2025) to ~2% (Aug–Sep 2026), with Q2 FY27 comp guidance of flat to +2% (FactSet StreetAccount, 14-Sep-2026). The market is pricing a western-wear fad peaking and retail-margin mean reversion: EPS fell 10% and 14% in FY2022–23 after the FY2021 boom. The doubt is **partly right** — comps are cyclical and fashion-sensitive — but **mostly wrong on the 5-year view**: ~13% annual square-footage growth funded from cash flow, 40%+ exclusive brands and a broad geographic white space mean EPS can grow even on flat comps. At 12x NTM, the market is effectively capitalising today's store base.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Bar: 1.30 × $9.16 = **$11.90** (FactSet consensus FY1). FY3 consensus is already $11.71. Sensitivity (estimate): FY1 sales $2.61bn growing 10%/yr ≈ $4.2bn in FY2031; at a 12% EBIT margin EPS ≈ $12.4 (pass), at 11% ≈ $11.4 (fail). So the test holds unless margins fall back toward the 10-yr average (10.5%) or unit growth stalls.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | P(causes fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Western-wear fashion cycle reverses (FY2022–23 style, comps −5 to −10%) | 25% | −20–30% vs path | 12% |
| US consumer recession in window | 35% | −15% temporary, recovers | 6% |
| New-store productivity fades / cannibalisation near 1,000 stores | 15% | growth halves | 6% |
| Merch-margin reversal (tariffs on exclusive-brand sourcing, promotions) | 20% | −150–250 bps EBIT | 6% |

Survival = 0.88 × 0.94 × 0.94 × 0.94 = 0.73. Every rolling 5-yr window since FY2015 rose ≥1.3x (FactSet actuals), but that started from a much lower margin. **P ≈ 73% → FAIL** (cyclical discretionary retail; calibration <80%).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +7% in every one of the next 5 years)
- Recurring revenue: none (store retail); pricing power modest; volume sensitivity high (FY2016 −20%, FY2022 −10%, FY2023 −14%).
- History: 7/10 years ≥7% (FactSet actuals), all three misses in non-recession or post-boom years.
- Weak year: new stores add ~9 pts of growth, but a −8% comp with deleverage would still cut EPS; buybacks are too small (~1 pt) to fill the gap.

P(no recession in 5 yrs) 0.60 × P(normal year ≥7%) 0.75^5 (0.24) = 0.14, plus ~2% for clearing a mild recession = **~16% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether western/work wear is a durable lifestyle category or a cycle at its peak. If comps hold positive through a soft consumer patch while new-store yields stay above 25% cash-on-cash, that would raise Test 2 toward 80%. Two consecutive negative-comp quarters with merch margin down would cut it to ~60%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted ≈ GAAP (5-yr gap −0.7%, FactSet). **FCF conversion is low**: 0.26 (5y) and 0.38 (10y) (FactSet). Capex of $179m vs EBITDA−EBIT of ~$79m in FY2025 (FactSet) reflects growth capex for new stores, plus inventory build. This is a growth-investment drag, not an accounting issue, and it limits buyback support in a downturn.
