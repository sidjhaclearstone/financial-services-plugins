## 3. What the market is doubting
At 14.5x, below POOL's historic 25–30x range (estimate), the market doubts (a) when new-pool construction and remodel recover, given high borrowing rates and weak discretionary spending (mgmt: "not yet observed a positive inflection", Feb-2026), (b) whether 2021–22 margins were a peak that resets lower, and (c) competitive intensity as SRS/Heritage (Home Depot) and distressed retailer Leslie's fight for share. The cyclical doubt is **temporary**: maintenance (about two-thirds of sales, company disclosure, approximate) proved resilient and the installed base keeps growing. The doubt about long-term margin and competition is **partly right**. The stock fell from ~$232 (Apr-2026) to $168.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY1+5 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Bar: 1.30 × $10.96 = **$14.25** in FY2031, which needs 5.4%/yr from FY1. FY3 consensus is $12.80, so FY3–FY6 still needs 3.6%/yr. EPS is still 42% below its 2022 peak of $18.43, which gives some recovery optionality.

Arithmetic: P(fail) ≈ 12.5% + 12% + 7.5% + overlap adj (−10%) + other ~3% ≈ **25%**. **P(Test2) ≈ 75% → FAIL.** Exposure to housing and discretionary spending keeps it under the 80% anchor.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of next 5 years)
- FY1 (2026, ~three-quarters reported) consensus is +2.2%. The high estimate of $11.14 (+3.8%) is below the 7% bar of $11.48, so **P(FY1 ≥ 7%) ≈ 2%**.
- History: 7/10 years ≥7%, with three consecutive declines in 2023–25.
- Recurring maintenance is about two-thirds of sales, but EPS is levered to discretionary volume and margin.
- **P(Test3) ≈ 2% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether 2025–26 is the trough of the post-COVID normalisation, or whether a lower-rate, lower-margin, more competitive structure is now permanent. Two signals would lift Test 2 toward 85%: permits turning up with Fed cuts, and gross margin holding ~29–30% against SRS/Heritage. Evidence of share loss to Home Depot's network, or of FCF staying below $300m (buybacks then funded by debt), would push it toward 65%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted vs GAAP gap is −2.1% (5y avg), so clean. FCF conversion is 0.89 over 5y and 0.87 over 10y, above the 0.80 threshold, but FY2025 FCF fell to $125m (2.0% yield) on working-capital/inventory build. In 2025, $531m of buybacks plus dividends exceeded FCF, and net debt rose to $1.43bn (FactSet Fundamentals). Flag: **shareholder returns funded partly by debt in FY2025**. No structural issue.
