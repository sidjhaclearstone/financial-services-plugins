## 3. What the market is doubting
At 10.5x the market doubts the durability of recruiting. The life-licensed sales force is guided flat to −2% for 2026 and term policies issued are down mid-single digits (FactSet SA, Aug-2026), which slows future premium growth. Other doubts: ISP earnings are equity-market sensitive; the multi-level-style distribution carries reputational/regulatory risk; and life insurers trade on low multiples generally. The stock fell to ~$233 after Q4-25 results (FactSet SA headline) and has since recovered to $280.84 (FactSet close 19-Oct-2026). Verdict: **partly temporary**. Recruiting is cyclical (cost-of-living pressure) and the 2027 50th-anniversary convention is a recruiting catalyst. But premium growth will lag history.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $32.85)
From the FY3 consensus ($29.97) EPS needs only 3.1%/yr in FY2029–31. Buybacks alone provide ~5%.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | Fail contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales-force shrinkage drives in-force premium growth to ~1% | 25% | growth falls to ~4–5% | 6% |
| Equity bear market at the FY2031 measurement point (ISP) | 15% | −5–7% total EPS | 4% |
| Regulatory action against distribution model / fiduciary rules on ISP | 5% | −10–20% | 5% |
| Mortality or credit shock in investment portfolio | 3% | −10% | 3% |

Survival = 0.94 × 0.96 × 0.95 × 0.97 = 0.83 → **P(Test2) ≈ 83% — FAIL**. The business is strong, but the flat recruiting trend is one credible threat and ISP adds cyclical exposure.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% every year FY1–FY5)
History: 9/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). The miss was 2022 (−1.5%, pandemic mortality), and 2023 was flattered by accounting. FY1 +10.3% is near-certain (P 0.97). FY2 consensus is only +7.7% (P ≈ 0.6). FY3–5 normal years P ≈ 0.75 each; P(equity/economic shock year in FY3–5) ≈ 0.4, with P ≈ 0.35 of hitting 7% that year. P ≈ 0.97 × 0.6 × [0.6 × 0.75³ + 0.4 × 0.35 × 0.75²] = 0.58 × 0.33 ≈ **19–20% — FAIL**. This is consistent with the insurer anchor of ≤25%.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Recruiting and licensing trends after the 2027 convention. A return to 3–5% sales-force growth would restore ~5% premium growth and push P(Test2) to ~88%. A second year of decline would cut it to ~75%.
