Specialness (PM Part 1 test): Good–track for dislocation. Two of the attributes apply:
- **A compelling customer value proposition**: simple, low-face-amount protection products sold through captive agencies and direct-to-consumer to an under-served lower-middle-income market.
- **Excellent capital allocation**: shares have fallen 4.2%/yr for ten years, with zero acquisitions.

There is no secular growth driver and no oligopoly.

## 3. What the market is doubting
At 10.3x NTM, the market still discounts three things:
- **The April-2024 short-seller allegations** about agency practices at American Income Life.
- **Agent-recruiting volatility.**
- **Lower direct-to-consumer sales**, as AI-driven search changes raise advertising costs. DTC is guided down single digits for 2026, and the stock fell $14 on the Q2-26 call (FactSet news).

Assessment: the regulatory doubt has largely been **proven wrong**. The SEC closed its investigation without recommending action on 24-Jul-2025, and the DOJ (WD Pa.) closed its investigation on 28-Jul-2025 (FactSet news). An EEOC matter remains. The DTC and advertising doubt is **real but modest**, because DTC is the smaller channel. The structural discount on US life insurers (rate and mortality model risk) is **partly right**.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in EVERY one of the next 5 years)
- **History:** EPS grew ≥7% in 7 of the last 10 years (FactSet). The misses were 2016 (+6.7%), 2020 (+1.9%) and 2021 (−0.3%, COVID mortality).
- **Recession resilience:** recurring revenue is very high (renewal premiums are ~90%+, estimate), demand is non-cyclical and buybacks provide ~4.5 pts. GL is fairly recession-resistant but mortality-sensitive.
- **FY2 problem:** consensus for FY2 (2027) is only +4.9%; the high estimate of 16.85 is barely above the 7% hurdle of 16.83. P(FY2 ≥7%) ≈ 20%.
- **Other years:** P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0.75 (consensus +8.3%, std 0.08). P(FY3–FY5 each ≥7%) ≈ 0.7³ ≈ 0.34, with a mortality-event haircut.

Overall ≈ 0.75 × 0.20 × 0.34 × 0.9 ≈ 5%, rounded up for buyback flexibility to **≈8% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether American Income can keep growing agent count and sales without its agency practices drawing renewed regulatory or reputational attack. That growth sets the premium-growth leg of the stack.
- **Upgrade:** two years of mid-single-digit agent and sales growth, plus EEOC closure.
- **Downgrade:** agent count shrinking, or a state or federal action on sales practices.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted vs GAAP gap is 3.3% on a 5-year average (FactSet), so there is no flag. FCF conversion is not meaningful for an insurer. The reported 1.53 reflects the timing of premium collection versus reserve build. The 2023 LDTI adoption raised reported earnings without a change in cash economics, so the 10-year CAGR overstates underlying growth.
