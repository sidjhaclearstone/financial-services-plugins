Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation** — a compelling customer value proposition ("OE FIX" parts that fix factory design flaws, broad catalogue) and a needs-based secular tailwind from an ageing vehicle parc. However, ROIC is only ~11%, the business depends on a few large auto-parts retailers, and it is acquisitive. That is good, not special.

## 3. What the market is doubting
At 13.3x, investors doubt (a) that FY2024–25 margins (16.8%, EPS +57% then +24%) were clean rather than inflated by tariff pass-through timing. FY1 EPS is now expected to fall. They also doubt (b) customer concentration: a few large retailers (AutoZone, O'Reilly, Advance Auto Parts) dominate sales, and Advance is distressed. Then (c) heavy-duty and specialty-vehicle cyclicality (freight weakness, softer consumer powersports demand), and (d) the long-run EV mix. The doubt about margin normalisation is **right near-term** (FY1 down). The secular doubt is **mostly wrong within five years**: the average vehicle age is at records and mgmt sees "no accelerating softness" in DIFM (Aug-2026).

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is the normalised EBIT margin once tariffs settle: ~15% (mgmt's 40% gross margin with flat SG&A) or a return to the 11–13% of 2019–23. Clean FY2027 growth of 6%+ with FCF conversion back above 80% would lift Test 2 toward 82%. A customer bankruptcy or a new tariff shock would cut it below 65%. Coverage is thin (FY3 n=2), so confidence is limited.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag.** The adjusted vs GAAP gap is 19.4% on a 5y average and 33.6% in FY2025 (adj $8.87 vs GAAP $6.64; FactSet). The gap comes from amortisation of acquired intangibles (SuperATV, Dayton Parts) and FY2025 unusual items: FactSet EPS before unusuals was $8.0, so about $1.4 of the gap is "unusual". FCF conversion is **0.66 over 5y and 0.72 over 10y**, persistently below 80%. The causes are inventory builds (FY2022 FCF $4m), receivables, and supply-chain-financing/factoring dynamics with large retail customers. FY2025 FCF was only $76m against $204m of net income. Earnings quality is below average.
