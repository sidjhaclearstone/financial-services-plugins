## 3. What the market is doubting
At 12.9x near a 10y EPS peak, the market doubts that $9.92–11 of EPS is through-cycle: CFR is strongly asset-sensitive (large securities/cash book, high non-interest-bearing deposits) and earnings fell 25% in 2020. Deposit competition is intensifying (beta drifting to low-40%), CRE lending is competitive on structure (Q2-26 Q&A). The doubt is right on cyclicality but partly wrong on the level: de novo expansion is now adding ~$0.45/share and rates are currently rising, not falling.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ 7% in EVERY one of next 5 years)
History 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), including −25% in 2020, −1% in 2019 and −3% in 2024. Spread income is rate-dependent; buybacks (~1%) cannot offset a cut cycle. Consensus FY3 is already +6.3%. P ≈ P(FY2 ≥7%) 0.65 × P(FY3 ≥7%) 0.40 × 0.5^3 ≈ 3%; plus estimate error → **P(Test3) ≈ 4% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The 2027–31 policy-rate path. If rates stay ≥3.5% and expansion markets keep adding ~$0.40+/share per year, EPS could compound ~8%; a return toward ZIRP would erase several years of growth. A demonstrated fall in rate sensitivity (hedging, fixed-rate asset growth) would lift P(Test2) to ~80%.
