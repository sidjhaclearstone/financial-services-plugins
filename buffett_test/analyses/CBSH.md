## 3. What the market is doubting
At 12.1x NTM, the market prices CBSH as an ordinary regional bank. It doubts three things:
- **Growth after peak rates.** Revenue jumped 28% in 2023 as rates rose, and falling rates now pressure the asset-sensitive NIM.
- **Low organic growth.** The franchise is mature and Midwest-based.
- **The first M&A in decades.** FineMark (closed Jan-2026) adds integration risk; systems conversion is due in Q4-2026 (FactSet transcript).

Assessment: the doubt is **partly right**. The 2019–2023 flat patch shows that CBSH needs a rate or credit tailwind to beat ~7% in a given year. The doubt is **wrong** about franchise quality: non-accrual loans are 0.05% of loans, ROA is 1.62% (Q1-26, FactSet news) and ROE has averaged 14.5% over ten years (FactSet). Q1-26 EPS was $0.96 vs $0.82 consensus (FactSet news).

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS in FY1+5 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
The target is FY2031 EPS ≥ 5.52 (1.30 × 4.249). FY3 consensus is already 5.05, so only ~3.0%/yr is needed over FY2028–31. Rolling 5-year history: five of six windows rose ≥30%. The exception was FY2018→23 (+23%), when the 2018 tax step-up inflated the base.

P(fail) ≈ 0.22, so **P(Test2) ≈ 78% → FAIL** (bar is 90%). The bank's earnings are cyclical, which the calibration caps below ~80%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in EVERY one of the next 5 years)
- **History:** EPS grew ≥7% in 6 of the last 10 years (FactSet). EPS fell in 2019, 2020 (−14.7%), 2022 and 2023.
- **FY1:** FY2026 consensus is +5.2%. With three quarters reported, the consensus std is only 0.087, so the ≥7% hurdle (4.32) is ~0.85σ above the mean. P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 20%.
- **Later years:**
  - Recurring revenue is moderately high: NII plus fees, with trust fees ≈ recurring.
  - Pricing power is limited (it is rate-driven).
  - Volume is sensitive to the credit cycle.
  - Buybacks give only ~1.8 pts of support in a weak year.

P(FY2–FY5 each ≥7%) ≈ 0.75 × 0.7 × 0.6 × 0.5 ≈ 0.16, with a recession likely inside the window.
Overall ≈ 0.20 × 0.16 ≈ **3% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether CBSH can grow EPS high-single-digit without a rate tailwind: fee growth plus FineMark wealth, and buybacks, against NIM compression.
- **Upgrade:** two years of ≥9% EPS growth in a falling-rate environment, with FineMark lifting trust-fee growth into double digits.
- **Downgrade:** a FineMark write-down, or NIM compression pushing FY2027 growth below 5%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted EPS equals GAAP (5-year average gap 0.0%, FactSet), so there is no flag. FCF conversion is not meaningful for a bank. Reserve releases flattered 2021 (EPS +55.7%), and 2020 was a reserve build year. No acquired-intangible amortisation distorted the series before 2026.
