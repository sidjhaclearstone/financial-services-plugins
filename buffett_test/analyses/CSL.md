## 3. What the market is doubting
At 14.2x the market doubts that peak-ish margins (20.4% vs 16.1% 10y avg, FactSet) hold while new non-residential construction is falling and input costs (oil-linked) are rising; FY2025 EPS fell 4% (FactSet) and FY2025 guidance was cut to flat revenue with EBITDA margin -250bp (company, Oct-2025). Assessment: **partly right.** Re-roofing demand and the consolidated competitive structure (rational capacity additions per the CEO) protect pricing better than in the 2010s, and the buyback is powerful at a 6.5% FCF yield, but margin is cyclical and the 2021–24 step-up included one-off price/cost catch-up.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Target 1.30 × 21.07 = 27.39 (FactSet consensus FY2026). FY3 (FY2028) consensus is 27.32 — the bar is roughly consensus three years out, then flat for three more years. A 10%/yr path gives 33.9 in FY2031 (24% cushion).

| Threat | Prob. | EPS impact |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial construction recession hitting around FY2030–31 | 20% | -20 to -30% (2020: -28%, FactSet) |
| Pricing discipline breaks (new TPO capacity, GAF/Holcim/JM competition) | 12% | -15 to -25% |
| Input-cost spike (petrochemicals) not passed through | 8% | -10% |
| Capital-allocation misstep (large acquisition at peak) | 5% | -5 to -10% |

Weighted by chance of breaching the cushion: 20%×0.7 + 12%×0.6 + 8%×0.3 + 5%×0.2 ≈ 25%; overlap with margin mean-reversion and leverage adds ~5% → **P(Test2) ≈ 70% → FAIL.** Anchor: cyclical exposure → usually < 80%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in every one of the next 5 years)
History: only 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet: 2017 -16.6%, 2020 -28.4%, 2023 -22.4%, 2025 -4.0%). Re-roofing is recurring but not contractual; volumes swing with weather, destocking and new construction; buybacks (~6%) cushion but cannot offset a 20% margin squeeze. FY2026 consensus +8.6% (std 0.31) — year 1 is close to the line.
Arithmetic: P(year 1 ≥7%) 0.75 × P(next four normal years each ≥7%) 0.8⁴ = 0.41 × P(no construction downturn in 5 yrs) 0.4 → 0.12, plus small chance of growing through a mild downturn (+0.02) → **≈ 12% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Durability of the post-2021 margin step-up: is 20%+ EBIT margin the new through-cycle level (oligopoly pricing, re-roof mix) or a peak? A full non-residential downturn with margins holding ≥18% would argue for Special status and P(Test2) ~85%.
