## 3. What the market is doubting
Q2-26 brought an EPS guidance cut. Revenue was up only "mid to high single digits" and the shortfall "goes through to the bottom line" (CEO, Q2-26 call). The shares fell ~5% that day to $161.16 and now sit at $148.10 (FactSet close). The specific doubts:
- Middle East disruption with "no near-term recovery anticipated", and UK softness.
- Lumpy large engagements rolling off.
- Margin dilution from heavy senior hiring.
- US competition consulting losing share in routine merger reviews (Q1-26 call).
- AI's two-sided effect: more complexity work, but pressure on the Technology/e-discovery segment and on the junior-leverage model.
- FY2025 FCF of only $94m (FactSet).
The doubt is **largely temporary** on the regional and timing issues. It is **partly right** on AI and talent risk, which are the structural questions for a people business.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $12.05)
FY1 $9.27 → $12.05 needs 5.4%/yr. FY3 2028 consensus is already $12.05 (n=2) (FactSet consensus), so on consensus the target is met three years early. History supports it: 2019→2025 adj EPS grew 7.3%/yr through COVID and the 2022 flat year (FactSet).

Survival = 0.92 × 0.925 × 0.925 × 0.955 × 0.98 ≈ 0.74. The counter-cyclical restructuring mix offsets a recession, and a buyback cushion of ~3.5 pts/yr helps, which gives **≈75% → FAIL** (and not a near-miss).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥7% in every one of the next 5 years)
- FY1 2026 consensus is +5.0% (range +4.8% to +5.5%) (FactSet consensus), so year one almost certainly fails.
- History: 6/10 years ≥7%, with near-zero growth in 2017, 2020 and 2022 and +3.6% in 2024.
- Only some revenue is recurring (economic consulting and litigation retainers). Results are lumpy, even though the mix is recession-resilient.
**≈2% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether the senior hiring wave (MD headcount +6–7%) converts into utilisation and margin by 2027–28, as FY2/FY3 consensus (+17%/+11%) assumes, and whether FCF conversion recovers. Two consecutive quarters of margin recovery with FCF back above 90% of adjusted NI would raise confidence. Further guidance cuts would lower it.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted ≈ GAAP (5y average gap 2.8%, FactSet). FCF conversion is 0.99x over 10y and **0.81x over 5y** (FactSet), borderline against the 80% threshold. FY2025 FCF of $94m vs ~$290m of adjusted NI (≈32%, FactSet-derived) reflects working capital and forgivable loans to senior hires (cause is an estimate; forgivable-loan burdens were discussed on the Q1-26 call). **Flag: borderline FCF conversion** — the reported EPS overstates cash earnings in hiring-heavy years.
