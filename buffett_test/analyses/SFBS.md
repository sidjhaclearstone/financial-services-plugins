## 3. What the market is doubting
11.2x is a typical regional-bank multiple, not a specific dislocation. Specific doubts: (1) an elevated problem multifamily/CRE book (non-accruals ~1.1% of loans, estimate); (2) intense deposit competition for municipal and correspondent balances, a rate-sensitive funding base; (3) the 2023 earnings hit showed NIM fragility; (4) founder-CEO succession; (5) execution risk on the Houston build, the largest start-up in company history (FactSet SA, Oct-2026). Verdict: **mostly temporary/generic**. Q1-26 EPS was +33% y/y (FactSet transcript), and credit was described as stable to improving. But bank earnings are inherently cyclical.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% every year FY1–FY5)
History: 8/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), but 2023 was −14.7% and 2024 +6.1%. A rate/credit shock breaks the streak. FY1 +22% is near-certain (0.98). FY2 +9.8% has P ≈ 0.75. FY3–5 normal years P ≈ 0.7 each; P(recession in FY3–5) ≈ 0.4, with that-year P ≈ 0.1. P ≈ 0.98 × 0.75 × [0.6 × 0.7³ + 0.4 × 0.1 × 0.7²] = 0.735 × 0.225 ≈ **17% — FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The credit cycle and deposit-cost behaviour in the next rate-cut phase. Two more years of ≥10% loan growth with stable non-accruals, plus buyback execution, would lift P(Test2) to ~82–85%. A CRE-driven provision spike would take it toward 65%.
