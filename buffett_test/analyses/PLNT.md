# Planet Fitness, Inc. (PLNT-US) — Buffett driver analysis
As-of 2026-10-19 | NTM P/E 14.8 (FactSet, 19-Oct-2026) | Mkt cap $4.0bn (FMP, 19-Oct-2026) | RBICS Hospitality Services / Arts, Entertainment and Recreation Providers | Confidence: Medium
Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation**. The appeal is a compelling customer value proposition (the low-price, "judgement-free" gym) on top of an asset-light franchise model with secular growth in fitness. That value proposition is now being questioned: member growth stalled in 2026 and management paused the Black Card price increase (FactSet StreetAccount, 7-May-2026), so PLNT is not yet "Special".

## 3. What the market is doubting
The shares fell 24% on the May-2026 guidance cut (FactSet StreetAccount, 7-May-2026). NTM P/E is now about 14.5x versus a 5-year average of ~35x (FactSet StreetAccount, 12-May-2026). The doubts are: (a) net member growth has stalled, so comps rely entirely on rate; (b) $10 rivals advertise low headline prices with hidden fees, which blurs the value proposition; (c) 4.4x leverage limits how long buybacks can carry EPS. Our view: the doubt is **partly right**. In 2025 PLNT added ~1.1m net members (+10% net adds; FactSet transcript, 7-May-2026), so the stall is recent and management attributes it to marketing execution, which is fixable. But a franchisor that has to stop raising prices to protect joins has less pricing power than the bull case assumed.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $4.23)
FY1 $3.25 × 1.30 = $4.23, which equals FY3 consensus of $4.22 (FactSet). This requires ~5.4%/yr from FY2026, or roughly flat EPS after 2028 if consensus is right. FY3 dispersion is wide (low $3.30, high $5.13, n=9).

| Threat | Prob. (5y) | EPS impact | Contribution to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Structural member stagnation (value prop eroded, saturation) | 25% | growth falls to ~3–4% (fails the bar) | ~12% |
| Recession / closures (pandemic-type) in late window | 7% | −20%+ | ~4% |
| Refinancing / leverage squeeze stops buybacks | 15% | −2 to −3 pts/yr | ~5% |
| Franchisee economics hit (build costs) slows openings | 15% | −2 pts/yr | ~4% |

Failure ≈ 25% (with overlap) → **P(Test 2) ≈ 75%**. FAIL.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2027–FY2031)
History: 9/10 years ≥7% (FactSet). That record includes the 2020 collapse (−97.5%) followed by rebounds, so it overstates resilience. FY1 2026 consensus is +5.9%, already a sub-7% year. Revenue is subscription-like, which is good, but the member base is now flat-ish. A low-price gym held up in ordinary recessions, but leverage and SBC dilution make buyback support pro-cyclical.
- P(member growth recovers and holds) ≈ 50%; P(each year ≥7% | recovery) ≈ 0.70^5 = 0.17 → 0.08
- P(no recovery) 50% × ~0.06 → 0.03
- Plus a small allowance for buyback smoothing → **P(Test 3) ≈ 12%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether net member growth resumes under the new marketing agency and Black Card value additions (FactSet StreetAccount, 3-Jun-2026) without discounting. Two things would raise Test 2 above 85%: two consecutive quarters of positive net joins, and a reinstated Black Card price increase with no churn spike.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag (moderate).** The adj vs GAAP gap is 37% 5y avg but only 17% latest (FactSet). The gap is mainly purchase-accounting amortisation from Sunshine Fitness, Up-C/TRA items and SBC, and it is narrowing. FCF conversion is healthy at 1.44x 10y / 1.36x 5y (FactSet). Buybacks above FCF (1.38x FCF 10y, FactSet) were debt-funded through securitisation, which is the real quality issue.
