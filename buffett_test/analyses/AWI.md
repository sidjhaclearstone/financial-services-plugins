It is a **true oligopoly**: US mineral-fibre ceilings are effectively a duopoly with USG/Knauf, with Rockfon as a distant third, and pricing power has been demonstrated. ## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x)
The stock fell 17.23 on the Q4-25 print (Feb-2026) and 12.22 on Q1-26 (Apr-2026) (StreetAccount). At $167.40 it trades below the $182 level of Jul-2026. The market doubts four things:
- **Mineral Fibre volumes.** Hybrid work and office vacancy cap renovation demand, and the market is "flattish, sideways" (CEO, Jul-2025).
- **Price sustainability.** EPS growth depends on price at a time of mid-single-digit input inflation and margins near peak (91st percentile).
- **Architectural Specialties.** Growth there is acquisition-led and its margin is lower; "recent acquisitions require ramp-up" (Apr-2026).
- **CEO succession.** Management says strategy is unchanged under new leadership (Feb-2026).

Assessment: **partly right.** Volume is the weak leg. Pricing, however, has been demonstrated across the cycle: EPS grew 8/10 years and compounded 15.3% over 5y through the office downturn (FactSet). Bidding activity is stable and project values are "rising above inflation" (Apr-2026), and management sees "no recession concerns" in customer sentiment (Feb-2026). The 17.9x multiple prices in the cyclical exposure; it is not a disruption discount.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of next 5 years)
- History: **8/10** years at ≥7% (FactSet). The misses were 2016 (+1.8%) and 2020 (−24.1%). Mineral Fibre renovation demand is recurring but not contractual, and volumes swing with non-res activity. Buybacks of ~2.5 pts help but cannot offset a 2020-type year.
- FY1: +13.8%, low +12.7% ⇒ 0.97. FY2: +13.6%, low +6.9% ⇒ 0.85. Normal later years: 0.80 each.
- P(no recession in years 3–5) 0.55. In a recession year, P(≥7%) is 0.15.
- 0.97 × 0.85 × 0.80² × (0.55 × 0.80 + 0.45 × 0.15) = 0.97 × 0.85 × 0.64 × 0.51 ≈ 0.27. Haircut ×0.75 for input-cost or destocking years ⇒ **≈ 20% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether Mineral Fibre volumes have bottomed. If volume grows 1–2% on top of 5–6% AUV, EPS compounding is high-teens-safe. Two things would argue for Special status and P(Test2) ~82%: a non-res downturn in which Mineral Fibre EBITDA margins hold above 40% and AUV stays positive, and data-centre or energy-saving products growing to a material share. Signs of USG/Rockfon price competition would cut it to ~65%.

## 7. Earnings quality
No adjusted vs GAAP flag: the gap is 8.1% over 5y and 4.7% in FY2025 (FactSet). **Mild FCF flag.** FactSet fcf_conversion of 0.67 (5y/10y) uses FF_FREE_CF, which is after dividends. On CFO−capex, conversion was 0.76 in FY2025 ($246m ÷ ~$323m adjusted net income). Two things depress it. WAVE JV distributions in excess of earnings are classified in investing cash flow (company practice, estimate), and capex stepped up to $109m. Adjusted for WAVE, conversion is about 0.9 (estimate). This is not a quality concern.
