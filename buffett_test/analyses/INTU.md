## 3. What the market is doubting
The stock has roughly halved (StreetAccount headlines show $403 in Feb-2026 and $313 at the Sep-2026 Investor Day vs $325.62 now). Doubts: (1) AI agents (and LLM-native tax/bookkeeping tools) commoditise DIY tax prep and SMB accounting; (2) actual DIY tax share losses and a "strategic FY27 operational reset", with management declining to say whether re-acceleration shows in FY2027 or slips to FY2028 (StreetAccount, Aug/Sep-2026); (3) revenue growth stepping down to 9–10% (CFO). Assessment: **partly right, partly temporary.** The DIY share loss is real and the growth step-down is real, but EPS growth remains double-digit via margin and buybacks, SBC-inclusive accounting removes a quality objection, and the ecosystem (QuickBooks ledger + payroll + payments + ProTax + Credit Karma data) is harder to displace than a single tax-filing app.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2032 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Target: 1.30 × 22.95 = 29.84 (FactSet consensus). FY3 (FY2029) consensus is already 29.24, so after FY2029 only ~0.7%/yr is needed. What would make FY2032 EPS lower?

| Threat | Prob. | EPS impact |
|---|---|---|
| AI disintermediation of DIY tax + SMB bookkeeping forces price cuts | 15% | -20 to -30% vs path |
| Continued DIY share loss / failed "Big Bets" (execution) | 10% (partly overlaps above) | -10 to -15% |
| Government free filing (IRS Direct File–type) expands | 4% | -10% |
| Credit Karma / Mailchimp cyclical hit coinciding with FY2032 | 3% | -5 to -10% |
| Consensus basis error (FY1 overstated vs new definition) | 3% | lowers bar risk |

Σ ≈ 35% raw; only threats large enough to push FY2032 below a ~20% cushion matter (path at 10%/yr ≈ 36.9 vs 29.84 target = 24% cushion). Weighting by probability that impact exceeds the cushion: 15%×0.7 + 10%×0.5 + 4%×0.3 + 3%×0.3 + 3% ≈ 20%. **P(Test2) ≈ 80% → FAIL.** Consistent with anchor "good business with one credible threat ≈ 70–85%".

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in every one of the next 5 years)
History: 9/10 years ≥7% (FactSet adjusted); the one miss (FY2026) is the SBC definition break — GAAP grew 9–31% in 9 of 10 years, with FY2022 GAAP -3.7% (Mailchimp amortisation). FY2020 (COVID) adjusted EPS +16.4%. Recurring revenue is high (subscriptions + annual tax filing), tax demand is recession-proof, but Credit Karma and Mailchimp are cyclical (Credit Karma revenue fell in FY2023) and new-user acquisition is now weak. Buybacks (~2% net) support a weak year.
Arithmetic: P(each normal year ≥7%) 0.85; P(recession inside 5 yrs) 0.5 with P(≥7% that year) 0.6 → 0.85⁴ × (0.5×0.85 + 0.5×0.6) = 0.522 × 0.725 = 0.38; haircut ×0.8 for AI/DIY reset uncertainty → **≈ 30% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether AI is a feature Intuit sells (agents embedded in QuickBooks/TurboTax, raising ARPC) or a substitute that erodes price. Evidence to watch: TurboTax unit share in the 2027 filing season, QuickBooks online customer growth and ARPC, and whether FY2027 non-GAAP EPS (SBC-inclusive) grows ≥ high-teens. Two filing seasons of stabilised DIY share would lift P(Test2) to ~88–90%.
