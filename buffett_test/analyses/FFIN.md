## 3. What the market is doubting
14x is a premium for a bank, so the market is not doubting quality; it doubts growth. FY2026's +15% is a NIM recovery from the 2023 trough; after that consensus slows to ~5–6%. EPS of $1.77 in FY2025 was only 8% above FY2022's $1.64. The doubt is largely right: this is a high-quality ~6% grower whose excess capital is not returned via buybacks.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × 2.038 = $2.65)
Needs 5.4%/yr from FY1; after consensus FY3 ($2.27) it needs 5.3%/yr for FY2029–31.

Failure ≈ 0.30×0.5 + 0.20×0.4 + 0.20×0.5 + 0.10×0.3 ≈ 0.15 + 0.08 + 0.10 + 0.03 = 36%, overlap-adjusted to ~33%. **P(Test2) ≈ 67% → FAIL.**

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ 7% in EVERY one of next 5 years)
Spread income is not recurring in the Test-3 sense; the history shows 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet), with −14% in 2023 and +3% in 2022 even without a recession. Consensus FY2 (+4.8%) and FY3 (+6.4%) are already below 7%. P ≈ P(FY2 ≥7%) 0.30 × P(FY3) 0.35 × 0.5^3 ≈ 1.3%; round up for estimate error → **P(Test3) ≈ 3% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The rate path: a sustained higher-for-longer curve plus 6–7% loan growth could push EPS ≥7%/yr; a renewed cutting cycle would stall EPS as in 2022–24. Initiating meaningful buybacks (capital ratios well above requirements, per AGM) would add ~1–2 pts and would be the main upgrade trigger.
