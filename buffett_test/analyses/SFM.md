## 3. What the market is doubting
Shares fell 26% on the Q3-2025 print (StreetAccount) and trade at 10.9x NTM. Doubts: (1) the 2024–25 margin surge (new-customer influx, GLP-1/health trend tailwinds) is lapping and comps have turned slightly negative; (2) affordability pressure among less-engaged, lower-income customers; (3) competition as mainstream grocers copy the assortment. Assessment: **partly right.** New-store returns and e-commerce remain strong and the buyback is large, but EBIT margin of 7.8% vs a 5.7% 10y average (FactSet) is the key exposure; a reversion to ~6.5% would cut EPS ~15% (estimate).

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Target 1.30 × 5.54 = 7.21 (FactSet consensus FY2026). FY3 (FY2028) consensus 6.51 → needs a further 3.5%/yr in FY2029–31.

| Threat | Prob. | EPS impact |
|---|---|---|
| Margin reverts toward 10y average (competition, promo, wage/fuel costs) | 25% | -15 to -25% vs path |
| Comp stagnation / format fatigue erodes new-store productivity | 12% | -10 to -20% |
| Competitive response (Walmart/Aldi/Costco health assortment) | 8% (overlaps) | -10% |
| Food-safety or execution event | 3% | -5% |

Path at 8%/yr from 5.54 gives 8.14 in FY2031 — only a ~13% cushion. Weighted breach probability: 25%×0.8 + 12%×0.6 + 8%×0.4 + 3%×0.2 ≈ 31%; overlap credit +3% → **P(Test2) ≈ 65% → FAIL.**

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in every one of the next 5 years)
History: 7/10 years ≥7% (FactSet; misses 2016 -3.5%, 2019 -3.1%, 2021 -15.7%). Grocery demand is recession-resistant and buybacks help, but FY2026 consensus is +4.4% (std 0.07, n=17) with the year largely reported — year 1 almost certainly misses.
Arithmetic: P(FY2026 ≥7%) ≈ 0.03 × anything thereafter → **≈ 2% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Through-cycle EBIT margin. If 2027 comps recover with margins ≥7.5% (loyalty, private label, vendor funding), consensus 9%+ growth plus ~6% buyback makes P(Test2) ~80%; a slide toward 6% would make the stock a value trap despite unit growth.
