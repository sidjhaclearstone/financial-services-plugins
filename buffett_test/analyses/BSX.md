## 3. What the market is doubting
At ~13–14x the stock trades at what analysts call "10 year trough" multiples (Leerink via StreetAccount, Apr-2026), against target multiples of 22–32x a year earlier. Three doubts:
1. **Watchman**: management said the "US market has slowed sharply and unexpectedly, primarily driven by compounding clinical evidence, which has impacted referral patterns." Global Watchman growth guidance was cut to flat/low-single-digit, with US H2-26 down mid-to-high single digits (Q2-26 call, StreetAccount, 29-Jul-2026).
2. **EP/PFA**: "we did not anticipate the degree of competitive share movement" in the US (same call).
3. **2027**: management expects revenue growth below market and "limited adjusted EPS growth" in 2027, with improvement "meaningfully in 2028."
This doubt is **right about 2026–27**: consensus FY2 is +4.2% (FactSet). Whether it is **temporary** depends on 2028 catalysts. The new clinical evidence on LAAC makes the Watchman issue look partly structural, not just a matter of timing.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $4.22)
FY1 (2026) consensus $3.25 → target $4.22 needs 5.4%/yr; from FY3 consensus $3.74, only 4.2%/yr for 2028–31. The consensus path FY1→FY3 is +7.2%/yr (FactSet consensus).

Survival = 0.85 × 0.86 × 0.925 × 0.955 × 0.96 ≈ 0.62. Adding back ~3 pts because the threats overlap (Watchman and EP weakness are correlated, so the product double-counts) gives **≈65% → FAIL**. A good business with more than one live threat sits below the 70–85% band.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥7% in every one of the next 5 years)
- FY1 2026 consensus +6.2% (range +1.6% to +10.1%) and FY2 2027 +4.2% (FactSet consensus). Two of the five years already fail on consensus, and management has guided to "limited" 2027 EPS growth.
- Recession year: procedures are mostly non-discretionary, but 2020 EPS fell 39% (FactSet), so elective deferral hurts. There is no buyback support.
- History: 8/10 years ≥7%, but the only real stress year (2020) failed.
P ≈ P(2026 ≥7%) 0.3 × P(2027 ≥7%) 0.15 × P(2028–30 all ≥7%) 0.6 ≈ **3% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether the new LAAC clinical evidence permanently shrinks Watchman's addressable market, or only slows adoption. Signs that would improve the view: US Watchman returning to growth in 2027, stable US PFA share, and the $500m savings showing up as the guided margin expansion. Further guidance cuts for 2028 would push the business to "Not special".

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag: ADJ_GAAP_GAP.** Adjusted EPS exceeds GAAP by a 5y average of 134% (latest 58%; FY2025 adj $3.06 vs GAAP $1.94, FactSet). The gap mainly reflects amortisation of acquired intangibles from serial M&A, plus restructuring, litigation and deal charges (the split is an estimate). FCF conversion vs GAAP NI is 0.98x (10y) and 1.28x (5y). Against adjusted NI, FY2025 FCF $3.66bn / ($3.06 × 1,495m) ≈ 80% (FactSet-derived), in line with management's 70–80% target (investor day, StreetAccount). Acquisitions are a recurring use of cash, so the adjusted EPS overstates owner earnings.
