## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x)
The stock fell from $3,690 in Mar-2026 to $3,009 after the Q3-26 print, a one-day fall of 397.89 (StreetAccount). It now trades at $3,098. The market doubts four things:
- **EPS has stalled.** FY2024→FY2026 grew only +2.2%/yr (FactSet), held back by LIFO charges, peso FX and SG&A/mega-hub investment.
- **Operating margin is at a 10y low** of 18.3% (FactSet) because the faster-growing commercial business carries a lower gross margin.
- **FCF and buybacks are squeezed.** Capex is ~$1.6bn/yr, against ~$0.5bn historically (Q3-26 guidance and FactSet). FY2025 buybacks were $1.58bn, versus $3–4bn in FY2021–24 (FactSet).
- **Longer-term fear** about EV parts intensity, and tariffs.

Assessment: **mostly temporary.** The investment cycle and LIFO are timing effects. Management targets margins of 18–19% and comps of ≥4% (Mar-2026 and May-2026). New mega-hubs "are outperforming expectations" (May-2026). Fleet ageing and the low EV share of the US car parc keep demand non-discretionary through 2031 (estimate). The doubt is **partly right** on one point: a lower FCF yield (4.0%) means slower share shrink than in the past decade.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of next 5 years)
- History: **8/10** years at ≥7% (FactSet), including FY2020 (+13.4%). The misses were FY2025 (−0.9%) and FY2026 (+5.3%). Demand is non-discretionary and buybacks add ~3.5 pts.
- FY1 (FY2027, just started): mean +12.0%, 1.3σ above the 7% bar ($163.2), low +3.8% ⇒ 0.80. FY2 +12.3% ⇒ 0.78. FY3–FY5 ⇒ 0.75 each. Inflation fading, weather and LIFO can each produce a sub-7% year, as in FY2025–26.
- Recession haircut is small: ×0.95.
- P ≈ 0.80 × 0.78 × 0.75³ × 0.95 ≈ **25% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether the FY2025–26 stall was purely an investment and LIFO timing effect. Two things would lift Test 2 toward 87% and Test 3 toward 35%: EBIT re-accelerating in FY2027 to +8–10% with margins stable at ~18.5%, and buybacks returning to over $2.5bn/yr. A margin below 17.5% or comps below 3% would argue for a structural mix problem and cut Test 2 toward 72%.

## 7. Earnings quality
No adjusted vs GAAP flag: the gap is −0.5% over 5y (FactSet). FCF conversion is 0.97 over 10y and 0.83 over 5y (FactSet), but fell to 0.72 in FY2025 ($1.79bn ÷ $2.50bn net income). The cause is the capex ramp, with ~$1.6bn/yr guided. This is **watch, not flag**: it is growth capex, not working-capital deterioration. Leases (stores) are an additional quasi-debt load on top of 2.75x net debt/EBITDA.
