## 3. What the market is doubting
Why 12.6x: (a) the pool cycle — new construction is tied to housing turnover and rates, and 2023 showed adj EPS −43% (FactSet); (b) tariffs and China sourcing (reduced, costs in guidance per FactSet StreetAccount, 25-Feb-2026); (c) competition from larger Pentair, and private-equity sponsor-overhang history; (d) adj EPS still below 2021. Our view: the doubt is **partly temporary**. Aftermarket replacement of pumps, filters and heaters is non-discretionary for the existing pool base, channel inventory is healthy (FactSet StreetAccount, 29-Apr-2026), and price is sticking. But the 2023 destock shows earnings swing violently with channel behaviour.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2027–FY2031)
History is thin. Adj EPS grew ≥7% in only 2 of 4 years (2022 −15%, 2023 −43%, 2024 +20%, 2025 +15%; FactSet). The 5/6 GAAP figure is distorted by the pre-IPO base. The recurring aftermarket helps, but dealer stocking cycles and weather create large yearly swings. Buybacks are currently small.
- P(no pool/housing downturn or destock in 5y) ≈ 40%; P(each normal year ≥7%) ≈ 0.75 → 0.40 × 0.24 = 0.09
- Downturn path ≈ 0.01
- **P(Test 3) ≈ 10%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is how much the installed-base aftermarket really insulates EPS in a downturn. We have only one cycle (2023) to judge, and it failed badly. Evidence that would change the view: a full housing slowdown with organic sales down less than 5%, together with sustained ~4% price, margins of 21%+ and a shift of FCF toward buybacks once net debt is <1.5x EBITDA.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag (moderate).** The adj vs GAAP gap is 50% 5y avg but 13% latest (FactSet). It reflects LBO-era acquired-intangible amortisation, IPO-related SBC and 2021–22 refinancing costs, and it is shrinking. FCF conversion is fine at 1.48x 10y / 1.11x 5y vs GAAP (FactSet), and FY2025 FCF of $226m is ~132% of adjusted NI (0.77 × 222.2m ≈ $171m, computed). Earnings quality is acceptable. The history is too short for high confidence.
