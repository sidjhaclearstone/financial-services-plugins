## 3. What the market is doubting (why <18x NTM / FCF yield 6.9%)
The shares fell from ~$86 in late January to $71 (StreetAccount price tags; FactSet close 19-Oct-2026). At 16x they trade well below the post-spin norm of low-to-high 20s (estimate). The doubts:
(a) **2026 is a lost year.** Consensus 2026 EPS is $4.04, flat on 2025 and below January's mid-single-digit guide (StreetAccount 28-Jan-2026).
(b) **China.** NE is down ~8% in 2026 and management no longer expects it to grow (StreetAccount 17-Sep-2026). China pricing is still weak.
(c) **Retention.** Maintenance price is being held back to protect retention.
(d) **Leverage and tariffs.** Negative equity at 2.9x ND/EBITDA, plus tariff-driven US project delays.

Our view is that the doubt is **mostly temporary**. NE is ~10% of operating profit, so most of the China damage is already in the base. The service annuity grows with the installed base, and modernisation of ageing units is a multi-year runway. The **right** part is that headline revenue growth is only ~3–5%. EPS compounding depends on mix, margin and buybacks, and 2026 shows that FX, interest and China can wipe out a year.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $5.25)
FY3 consensus is $5.03 (FactSet), so 2029–31 needs only ~1.4%/yr. What would make 2031 EPS lower?

| Execution/safety/reputational event | 3% | −10% | ~1% |

Σ ≈ 17%, less ~1 pt overlap → failure ≈ 16% → **P(Test 2) ≈ 84%**. FAIL, not a near-miss (6 pts short of 90%). The recurring service annuity puts it in the upper "good business with one credible threat" band. Execution misses (2026) and FX exposure keep it below the 90% anchor.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of FY2026–FY2030)
FY1 2026 consensus is −0.2%, and even the high estimate ($4.09) is only +1.0% (FactSet). P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0. History: 3/5 years ≥7% (2022 +5.3%, 2025 +5.7%) (FactSet). **P(Test 3) ≈ 1%**. FAIL. Even ignoring FY1, ~0.6 × 0.75^4 ≈ 0.19.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether 2027 delivers the +12% rebound consensus expects (NE positive ex China, modernisation in the teens, repair price). Two clean years of ≥8% EPS growth with retention stable would move P(Test 2) to ~88%. A second flat year would cut it to ~75%.

## 7. Earnings quality
The adj-vs-GAAP gap is 5.1% 5y avg but **15.6% latest** (FY2025 GAAP $3.50 vs adj $4.05, FactSet). This is a mild flag: adjusted EPS excludes restructuring (UpLift) and other charges, and the breakdown is not in our data. FCF conversion is 1.20x 10y / 1.07x 5y vs GAAP NI (FactSet). Against adjusted NI (~$1.60bn, computed) FY2025 FCF of $1.44bn is ~90%, which is acceptable.
