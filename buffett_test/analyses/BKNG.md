## 3. What the market is doubting
At 13.6x NTM for a business with 35% EBIT margins and 47% ROIC (FactSet FY2025), the market is pricing **AI-agent disintermediation**: LLM assistants (Google, OpenAI) moving the top of the funnel, raising customer-acquisition costs and pressuring take rates (StreetAccount, Feb-2026 Q4 takeaways). Secondary: Middle East disruption (3-pt Q2-2026 headwind) and travel cyclicality. Assessment: **mostly temporary/overstated, but not wrong to price.** LLM traffic is still <1% of room nights (Q2-2026 call, Aug-2026); Booking is partnering with Google and OpenAI and launched agentic booking in its own app; ~90% of the OTA market's supply is non-chain and still needs Booking's lower-funnel infrastructure (payments, customer service, inventory). The risk is a slow take-rate leak rather than a cliff.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Target 1.30 × 10.46 = 13.60 (FactSet consensus FY2026). FY3 (FY2028) consensus is already 14.42; a 12%/yr path gives 18.4 in FY2031, a ~26% cushion.

| Threat | Prob. | EPS impact |
|---|---|---|
| AI agents capture demand & squeeze take rate / raise marketing cost | 15% | -20 to -30% vs path |
| Severe travel shock in FY2031 (recession, pandemic, war) | 8% | -25 to -60% |
| Regulation (EU DMA, parity clauses, digital-tax/competition fines) | 5% | -5 to -10% |
| Execution / Asia competition (Trip.com) | 3% | -10% |

Weighted by chance of breaching the cushion: 15%×0.6 + 8%×0.8 + 5%×0.2 + 3%×0.2 ≈ 17%; add ~5% for overlap/unknowns and cyclicality → **P(Test2) ≈ 78% → FAIL.** Anchor: cyclical exposure usually < 80%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in every one of the next 5 years)
History: 9/10 years ≥7% (FactSet) but this flatters — 2021–2023 were rebound years and 2020 was -95%. Pre-COVID 2016–2019 all ≥10%; in 2008–09 Priceline grew EPS through recession on share gains (company history, estimate). Revenue is transactional, not recurring; volume is sensitive to macro and exogenous shocks; buybacks (~4%) and cost savings give support in a mild downturn, not a severe one. FY2026 consensus +14.6% (std 0.14, n=33) makes year 1 very likely.
Arithmetic: P(normal year ≥7%) 0.88; P(downturn/shock year within 5 yrs) 0.5 with P(≥7% that year) 0.35 → 0.88⁴ × (0.5×0.88 + 0.5×0.35) = 0.600 × 0.615 = 0.37; AI haircut ×0.7 → **≈ 25% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Share of bookings originated inside third-party AI agents and whether Booking keeps the merchant-of-record and payment economics there. Watch: direct-channel mix, marketing as % of gross bookings, take rate. If LLM-originated bookings scale while take rate holds, P(Test2) would rise to ~85%.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag: ADJ_GAAP_GAP** — adjusted vs GAAP gap 33.8% (5y) and 37.7% (FY2025) (FactSet: adj 9.12 vs GAAP 6.62). Likely drivers (estimate; not itemised in this data): FX remeasurement on euro-denominated debt, equity-investment marks, regulatory fines (e.g. Spanish competition fine), transformation/restructuring charges and SBC. FCF conversion 1.50x (10y) / 1.65x (5y) (FactSet) is flattered by merchant-model working capital (customer prepayments/deferred merchant bookings) and SBC; not a red flag while growing, but would reverse in a sharp volume decline (as in 2020, FCF -$0.2bn).
