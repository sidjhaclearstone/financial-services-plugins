Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation**. CarGurus is the most-visited US auto-listings marketplace. Two of the PM attributes apply: a two-sided network effect giving a compelling customer value proposition (deal ratings, record dealer retention per FactSet StreetAccount, 19-Feb-2026), and shareholder-friendly capital allocation (no net debt, heavy buybacks). ## 3. What the market is doubting
At 10.5x the market is pricing ex-growth. There are three doubts. (a) The EBIT margin is at a 10y peak of 27% after the CarOffer exit, and management is reinvesting. (b) Dealer profitability is under stress and vendors are being consolidated (FactSet transcript Q3-25, 6-Nov-2025). (c) AI/agentic search (Google, ChatGPT shopping) could disintermediate listings sites and erode the traffic moat. Our view: (b) is cyclical and CarGurus is gaining share as dealers consolidate onto fewer marketplaces (FactSet StreetAccount, 19-Feb-2026). (c) is the real, unresolved doubt. Direct traffic is up 16% y/y (same source), which is mildly reassuring, but the risk is genuine over five years.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $3.41)
FY1 $2.62 × 1.30 = $3.41, below FY3 consensus of $3.56 (FactSet). Even with zero revenue growth, buybacks alone at ~9.6% FCF yield would compound per-share earnings ~5–6%/yr if margins held (estimate).

| Threat | Prob. (5y) | EPS impact | Contribution to failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI/agentic disintermediation of listing traffic | 20% | −20 to −40% | ~12% |
| Margin mean-reversion from a 27% peak (competition, reinvestment) | 25% | −15% | ~6% |
| Auto-dealer recession late in window | 15% | −15% | ~3% |
| Capital misallocation (large M&A; mgmt flags "disciplined M&A") | 10% | −10% | ~3% |

Failure ≈ 24% → 25% (overlap) → **P(Test 2) ≈ 75%**. FAIL.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2027–FY2031)
History: 7/8 years ≥7% (FactSet), but the path is volatile: 2022 −35%, and margins swung between 3.6% and 27% (FactSet). Revenue is ~recurring dealer subscriptions, but dealers cut budgets in downturns. Pricing power is decent. Buybacks give strong support: at 10x P/E a weak year can still show EPS growth.
- P(no auto/dealer downturn in 5y) ≈ 55%; P(each normal year ≥7%) ≈ 0.80 → 0.55 × 0.33 = 0.18
- Downturn: P(still ≥7% via buybacks) ≈ 0.20 → 0.45 × 0.20 × 0.80^4 = 0.04
- **P(Test 3) ≈ 22% → 20%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether consumer car shopping keeps starting on listings marketplaces as AI agents mediate discovery. Two things would raise Test 2 toward 85%: evidence that CarGurus is a preferred data/inventory source for AI agents (licensing or integration), and steady revenue per dealer growth through the 2026 investment year with margins back to ~30% adj. EBITDA.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag.** Adj vs GAAP gap is 284% 5y avg, 47% latest (FactSet), well above 15%. Causes are SBC (the main recurring item), CarOffer impairments and wind-down losses 2023–24 (GAAP EPS $0.27/$0.20, FactSet), and acquired-intangible amortisation. FCF conversion is strong at 1.73x 10y / 1.88x 5y vs GAAP (FactSet) and ~116% of adjusted NI in FY2025 ($266m vs 2.28 × 100.4m ≈ $229m, computed). Cash earnings are real, but SBC is a genuine cost that buybacks partly just offset.
