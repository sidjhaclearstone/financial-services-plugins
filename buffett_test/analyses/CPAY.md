## 3. What the market is doubting
At 13.1x the market doubts (1) the quality of fleet-card growth (fees, fuel-price sensitivity, long-run EV transition eroding fuel-card volumes), (2) the acquisition-dependence of the EPS algorithm and integration risk, and (3) macro sensitivity of SMB spend and FX volatility. FY2026 consensus +28% EPS / +18% sales (FactSet) is flattered by acquisitions plus fuel and weak-dollar tailwinds (CFO, May-2026). Assessment: **mostly temporary/overdone.** Organic growth has been 10–11% for five straight quarters (CFO, May-2026), the mix is shifting toward corporate payments (cross-border targeted at ~$1.5bn revenue in 2026), lodging has stabilised, and 93.5% revenue retention shows stickiness. The fuel/EV and fee-regulation risks are real but slow.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Target 1.30 × 27.41 = 35.64 (FactSet consensus FY2026). FY3 (FY2028) consensus is already 37.10; a 12%/yr path gives 48.3 in FY2031 (~36% cushion).

Weighted by chance of breaching the cushion: 10%×0.5 + 7%×0.3 + 5%×0.4 + 5%×0.3 + 4%×0.2 ≈ 11%; add ~4% for overlap/unknowns → **P(Test2) ≈ 85% → FAIL (near-miss).** Anchor: good business with one credible threat ≈ 70–85%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in every one of the next 5 years)
History: 8/10 years ≥7% (FactSet; misses 2020 -5.9%, 2023 +5.1%). Revenue is largely recurring (fees on ongoing spend; 93.5% retention) but sensitive to fuel prices, FX and SMB activity; buybacks and acquisitions give management levers to keep EPS growing in a soft year, and FY2026 consensus +28% (std 0.16) makes year 1 near-certain.
Arithmetic: year 1 ≈ 0.97; P(each later normal year ≥7%) 0.85; P(recession in yrs 2–5) 0.45 with P(≥7% that year) 0.35 → 0.97 × 0.85³ × (0.55×0.85 + 0.45×0.35) = 0.97 × 0.614 × 0.625 = 0.37; haircut ×0.8 for fuel/FX shock and 2023-style integration year → **≈ 30% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
How much of the 20%+ EPS algorithm is truly organic versus acquisition/fuel/FX-driven, and whether corporate payments can keep 10%+ organic growth through a downturn. Two years of ≥10% organic growth with FX volatility normalising would lift P(Test2) to ~88–90%.
