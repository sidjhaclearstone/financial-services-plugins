## 3. What the market is doubting
At 10.9x, investors are pricing (a) peak HOKA, since growth has slowed from 20%+ to low double digits and running competitors (On, Asics) are strong, (b) the risk that UGG's fashion cycle turns, as it did in FY2016 (EPS −15%), and (c) margin pressure from Vietnam tariffs (Telsey estimated a ~$185m COGS hit, partly mitigated; Jul-2025) and from promotions. The doubt about margins is **partly right**: the 22.8% EBIT margin is near its peak. The doubt about the business looks **too harsh**. In FY2026 EPS still rose 10.9%, ROIC was 37%, the balance sheet holds $1.5bn of net cash, and the buyback alone delivers about 6%/yr.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY1+5 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Bar: 1.30 × $7.53 = **$9.79** in FY ending Mar-2032. FY3 consensus is already $9.34 (n=10), so the remaining requirement is only ~1.6%/yr from FY3 to FY6. **What would have to happen for EPS to be lower in five years?**
| Threat | P | EPS impact by FY6 | Fails bar? |
|---|---|---|---|
| HOKA brand fade or loss of share in running (fashion/competition) | 15% | −25% to −35% pre-buyback | Yes in most cases, even after ~30% share shrink |
| UGG fashion cycle down (repeat of FY2015–17) | 15% | −15% | Fails only if it coincides with HOKA slowing (overlap ~5%) |
| Tariff and margin reset to ~17–18% EBIT | 15% | −20% | Partly offset by buybacks; ~half fail |
| Recession year near FY6 | 15% | −10% to −15% | Rarely fails alone given the ~23% FY1→FY3 head start |

Arithmetic: P(fail) ≈ 15% (HOKA) + ~5% (UGG overlap) + ~7% (margin) + ~2% (recession) − overlap ~4% ≈ **25%**. **P(Test2) ≈ 75% → FAIL.** Fashion-driven discretionary demand caps this below the 80% anchor.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% in each of next 5 years)
- History: 9/10 years ≥7% (the miss was FY2016 at −15%; FY2019 just made it at +8.8%). FY2020 (COVID, to Mar-21) was +40.6%. That is a strong record, but it was built on margin expansion that has now run its course.
- FY1: consensus +7.3%, mean $7.53 against a 7% bar of $7.51, std $0.13, low $7.37 (+5%). Deckers usually beats, but P(FY1 ≥ 7%) ≈ 60%.
- Revenue is not recurring (wholesale plus DTC footwear). Pricing power is real but tested by tariffs. Volume is sensitive to the consumer. Buybacks add about 6 pts even in a weak year, which is the main support.
- P ≈ 0.60 (FY1) × P(no recession hit over FY2–5) ~0.75 × P(each normal year ≥7%) ~0.80^4 = 0.41 → 0.60 × 0.75 × 0.41 ≈ **18%**, rounded down for fashion risk to **15% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether HOKA can sustain double-digit growth (international mid-teens, US mid-single digits per mgmt, May-2026) without heavy promotion while UGG holds. Evidence of HOKA share gains in specialty run and a stable 20%+ EBIT margin through the tariff cycle would lift Test 2 toward 85%. Two years of falling HOKA DTC or UGG markdowns would cut it below 65%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Clean. Adjusted vs GAAP gap ≈ 0% over 5y (adj_vs_gaap_gap_5y_avg_pct −0.0). FCF conversion is 0.96 over 5y and 1.09 over 10y (FactSet Fundamentals).
