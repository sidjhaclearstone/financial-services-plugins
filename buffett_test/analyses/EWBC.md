- **Excellent management**: the same CEO for over 30 years, a best-in-class efficiency ratio, and one of the strongest tangible common equity (TCE) levels in the industry (FactSet transcript Jan-26).

## 3. What the market is doubting
At 11.0x NTM, the market applies a discount to EWBC for three reasons:
- **US–China geopolitical exposure**: Greater China branches, a cross-border customer base and sanctions/de-risking risk.
- **Commercial real-estate concentration.**
- **Asset sensitivity as rates fall.**

Assessment: the doubts are **partly right but over-discounted**:
- **Geopolitics:** this is a genuine tail risk that cannot be diversified away.
- **Credit:** the record argues against the CRE fear. EPS fell only 13.9% in 2020 (FactSet), and the bank went through the 2023 regional-bank stress without a loss.
- **Rates:** NIM pressure is real, but management expects dollar NII growth from balance-sheet expansion (FactSet transcript Jan-26).

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS grows ≥ 7% in EVERY one of the next 5 years)
- **History:** EPS grew ≥7% in 6 of the last 10 years (FactSet). It fell in 2019 (−4.2%) and 2020 (−13.9%), and grew only +3.3% in 2023 and +1.8% in 2024.
- **Revenue and pricing:** recurring spread revenue is high, but pricing power is rate-driven.
- **Weak-year support:** buybacks give only ~1 pt in a weak year.
- **Arithmetic:**
  - P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0.95 (consensus +12.4%, 2026 nearly complete).
  - FY2 (+7.8%) ≈ 0.55 and FY3 (+7.0%) ≈ 0.45.
  - FY4–FY5 ≈ 0.5 each.
  - A recession inside the window is likely.

Overall ≈ 0.95 × 0.55 × 0.45 × 0.5 × 0.5 ≈ 6%, plus correlation credit, gives **≈8% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is US–China relations. A sanctions or capital-control escalation would directly impair the cross-border franchise and deposit base. It is the one risk that conservative underwriting cannot offset.
- **Upgrade:** sustained 5–7% loan growth at a flat NIM through a rate-cutting cycle, plus a step-up in buybacks after Basel relief.
- **Downgrade:** any regulatory action on cross-border activity, or office CRE losses rising above 50bp.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted EPS equals GAAP (5-year average gap 0.0%, FactSet), so there is no flag. FCF conversion is not meaningful for a bank. Tax-credit investment amortisation ($70–80m guided for 2025, FactSet news) lowers the effective tax rate. It is a recurring but policy-dependent item.
