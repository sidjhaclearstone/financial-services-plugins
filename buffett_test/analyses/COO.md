## 3. What the market is doubting
At 12.4x NTM for an oligopoly consumable franchise, the market doubts management: CooperVision organic growth has fallen behind the MSD market (limited super-premium daily exposure, hydrogel rationalisation, China/Japan weakness); the CooperSurgical strategic review drew "robust buyer interest" (Jun-2026) yet management then defended keeping the unit (Sep-2026 Q&A), disappointing those expecting a sale-funded buyback; and FCF/adj. NI is only ~53%. The franchise doubt is mostly wrong (the industry is a stable four-player market growing MSD); the execution/capital-allocation doubt is partly right.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ 7% in EVERY one of next 5 years)
Recurring revenue is high (contact lenses are replenished consumables, majority of sales — estimate ~2/3 Vision), but: history 7/10 years ≥7% with FY2020 −22%, FY2022 −6% and FY2023 +3% (FactSet actuals); and consensus FY2 (FY2027) is only +3.6% — the series is expected to break in year 2. P(FY2027 ≥7%) ≈ 25% × P(other four years ≥7%) ≈ 0.6^4 = 0.13 → ~3%; allow upside from a buyback surprise → **P(Test3) ≈ 5% → FAIL.**

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether CooperVision returns to at-or-above-market organic growth (MyDay MiSight launches in Europe/Asia Pac, new dailies) and whether capital allocation shifts to buybacks. Evidence of 6%+ Vision organic growth plus a CooperSurgical sale funding buybacks would lift P(Test2) to ~85%.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Flag:** adjusted vs GAAP gap 61.5% 5y avg, 120% latest (FactSet) — amortisation of acquired intangibles (Generate Life Sciences and other fertility/surgical deals), restructuring and litigation charges (fertility litigation settled per Sep-2026 Q&A). **Flag:** FCF conversion 65% 10y / 44% 5y (FactSet) — capex $360–420m in FY23–25 (capacity build) and working capital; FY2025 FCF $434m vs adjusted NI ~$824m (53%). Adjusted EPS materially overstates owner earnings; GAAP ROIC 3.4% (FactSet FY2025) reflects goodwill-heavy acquisitions.
