## 3. What the market is doubting
At 9.1x NTM the market treats BLKB as a melting vertical-SaaS asset: (1) AI agents and horizontal CRMs (Salesforce, Bonterra) could erode a ~4% grower; (2) nonprofit sector stress from reduced government grants; (3) a history of GAAP losses, large SBC and a 2020 security incident; (4) leverage (gross debt/EBITDA 3.6x, FactSet FY2025). Shares traded at ~$36 in Jul-2026 (FactSet SA headline) before recovering to $53.24 (FactSet close 19-Oct-2026). Evidence leans "partly wrong": mgmt reports no material change in retention, >20% of customers on 4-year+ contracts, gross dollar retention target 91–92% by end-2027 (FactSet SA, Feb/Jul 2026). The AI doubt is real but unproven either way.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $6.82)
FY2 consensus is already $5.97, so EPS needs only ~3.4%/yr from 2027 to 2031 — less than the buyback yield alone.

What would have to happen for EPS to be lower?
| Threat | P | EPS impact | Fail contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI/CRM disruption breaks retention and pricing (GDR <88%) | 12% | growth falls to 0–2% | 12% |
| Nonprofit recession hits transactional/payments revenue near FY2031 | 20% | −5–10% that year | 4% |
| Capital-allocation misstep (large debt-funded M&A) or cyber/regulatory event | 5% | −10–20% | 5% |

Survival = 0.88 × 0.96 × 0.95 = 0.80 → **P(Test2) ≈ 80% — FAIL** (below 90%). One credible threat (AI) keeps it in the 70–85% band.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ +7% every year FY1–FY5)
EPS grew ≥7% in only 6/10 years (FactSet), with declines in 2019 (−13.5%) and 2022 (−11.5%). FY1 (+17.9%) is near-certain (three quarters reported). FY2 +13.8% is likely. Revenue is ~mostly recurring subscription, but the transactional share falls with giving in a recession, and margin expansion is front-loaded. P ≈ 0.85 (FY1–FY2) × [0.6 (no recession FY3–5) × 0.65³ + 0.4 × 0.25 × 0.65²] = 0.85 × 0.21 ≈ **18% — FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether AI turns the Blackbaud data moat into an upsell (Development Agent, AI catalogue not in guidance) or into churn. Reported GDR trending to 92% plus AI revenue in 2027 would raise P(Test2) toward 85–88%. A GDR slip or a large acquisition would cut it.
