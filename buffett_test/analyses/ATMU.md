## 3. What the market is doubting
The stock has fallen from ~$65 (Feb-2026) to $44 (FactSet close 19-Oct-2026; prices in StreetAccount headlines). Doubts: (a) truck/engine electrification eroding the long-run diesel filter pool; (b) margin already at a record 16.4% after inflation pricing; (c) heavy dependence on Cummins as first-fit customer and former parent; (d) input cost (plastics/petroleum) and tariff noise (Q1-26 Q&A). I judge (a) mostly wrong on a 5-year horizon — the aftermarket follows the installed base, which ages slowly — but (b) is right: margin expansion is largely spent. The cause of the further ~18% decline since the Aug-2026 call is not identified in the evidence pulled; data thin.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS ≥ 7% in EVERY one of next 5 years)
Aftermarket is the majority of sales (company description; share not quantified in the data pulled — estimate ~2/3), so revenue is semi-recurring, but first-fit is highly cyclical and price is only ~1–1.5%. History: only 3/5 years ≥7% (FactSet); FY2022 −0.7% and FY2023 +0.5% GAAP; no pre-2020 standalone data. Buybacks (~1%) cannot offset a freight recession. P(no recession/freight downturn over 5y) ≈ 50% × P(each normal year ≥7%) ≈ 0.75^5 = 0.24 → 12%; plus small chance of ≥7% in a mild downturn → **P(Test3) ≈ 12% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
Whether margins can hold ~16–17% EBIT (≈20% adj. EBITDA) through a freight cycle as a standalone company. Evidence of share gains sustained >2% p.a. and Koch delivering industrial-filtration growth would push P(Test2) above 85%; a price give-back or Cummins volume loss would cut it to ~60%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Adjusted vs GAAP gap 11.4% 5y avg (9.0% latest) — below the 15% flag (FactSet). FCF conversion 73% 5y / 81% over available history (FactSet) — **flag: 5y < 80%**, driven by FY2024 (FCF $49m) separation costs and working-capital build to stand up the wholly-owned distribution network, plus capex doubling ($26m → $54m). FY2025 conversion recovered to ~63% of GAAP NI ($132m / $207m), still soft.
