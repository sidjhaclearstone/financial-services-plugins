The defining engine is deleveraging. The company buys brands with debt and uses a ~10% FCF yield to repay it, so interest savings accrete to EPS.

## 3. What the market is doubting
At 10x NTM and a ~10% FCF yield, the market treats PBH as an ex-growth, levered brand roll-up. It doubts several things:
- organic growth: 1–3%, and FY2025 EPS fell 3%;
- Clear Eyes supply, where recovery is "gradual and multi-year";
- e-commerce order lumpiness;
- the re-levering to >4x for new acquisitions (FactSet transcripts, Aug/Sep-2026).

The doubt is **largely right about growth** and **probably wrong about durability**. The brands are needs-based and retailers support them. Consumer stress shows up as channel shift rather than private-label trade-down. EBIT margins have held in a 28–33% band for a decade.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(EPS FY2031 ≥ 1.30 × FY1 consensus)
Bar: 1.30 × $4.58 = **$5.96** (FactSet consensus FY1, year to Mar-2027). This needs 5.4%/yr against a driver stack of ~7.5%, so the cushion is thin. Base rate: all 6 rolling 5-yr windows since FY2015 achieved ≥1.3x (FactSet actuals), but the latest only just made it (1.35x) and the trend is decelerating.

| Threat | P | EPS impact | P(causes fail) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic stall or decline (Clear Eyes, private label, retailer destock) | 30% | −2–3 pts/yr | 12% |
| Acquisitions (e.g., Breethe) underperform; deleveraging slower than planned | 20% | −1–2 pts/yr | 7% |
| Refinancing at higher rates on >4x leverage | 15% | −1 pt/yr | 3% |
| OTC regulatory (FDA monograph) or tariff/supply shock | 10% | −5–10% | 3% |

Survival = 0.88 × 0.93 × 0.97 × 0.97 = 0.77. The strong historical base rate lifts it slightly, to **P ≈ 78% → FAIL**. Demand is defensive, but the growth margin over 5.4%/yr is too thin for ≥90%.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +7% in every one of the next 5 years)
FY1 consensus is already only **+4.7%**, and FY3 is +4.1% (FactSet consensus), so year 1 most likely fails the test outright.
- Recurring/needs-based demand is high and recession sensitivity low.
- Pricing power is limited, and growth is lumpy by design: acquisitions dilute first, then deleveraging accretes.
- History: 6/10 years ≥7% (FactSet actuals), and only 1 of the last 4 (+3.7%, 0.0%, +7.4%, −3.1%).

P(FY1 ≥7%) ≈ 0.15 × 0.6^4 (0.13) = 0.02, plus a little upside if consensus is too low. **P ≈ 4% → FAIL**.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key uncertainty is whether the new acquisitions plus Clear Eyes recovery lift organic growth to ≥3% while FCF of ≥$270m brings leverage back under 3x by FY2029. If that happens, EPS compounds ~8%, and Test 2 rises toward ~85%. If organic growth stays ≤1%, or another dilutive deal comes before deleveraging, Test 2 falls to ~65%.

## 7. Earnings quality
Good. Adjusted vs GAAP gap averages 4.8% over 5 years (12.1% latest, impairments; FactSet). FCF conversion is 1.63x (5y) and 1.51x (10y) (FactSet). It exceeds net income because tax-deductible intangible amortisation and capex are only ~1% of sales. No flag.
