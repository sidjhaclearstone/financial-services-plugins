Specialness (PM Part 1 test): **Good–track for dislocation**. GEHC sits in a global imaging oligopoly (with Siemens Healthineers and Philips), backed by a large recurring service base, pharmaceutical diagnostics, and secular demand from population ageing. Management is executing a credible margin plan. Growth is mid-single-digit, though, and capital allocation is unproven post-spin (Jan 2023), so not "Special".

## 3. What the market is doubting
Why 13.1x: (a) revenue growth has been low-single-digit (3.7% 5y, FactSet); Citi downgraded the stock until growth reaches mid-single-digit (FactSet StreetAccount, 7-Oct-2025); (b) China volume-based procurement and anti-corruption pressure; (c) tariffs and memory-chip cost inflation forced a 2026 EPS guide cut (FactSet transcript Q1-26, 29-Apr-2026); (d) adj EPS has been flat for three years since the spin. Our view: (c) is **temporary**, since price on new orders, VCP and freight changes are offsetting. (a) and (b) are **partly right**: GEHC is a mid-single-digit grower whose EPS growth depends on executing the margin plan.

## 4. TEST 2 — P(FY2031 EPS ≥ 1.30 × FY1 = $6.40)
FY1 $4.92 × 1.30 = $6.40 vs FY3 consensus $6.04 (FactSet). This needs ~1.9%/yr after 2028, or ~5.4%/yr from FY1.

## 5. TEST 3 — P(EPS +≥7% in every year FY2027–FY2031)
History: only 3/6 GAAP years ≥7% (FactSet) and 1/3 adj years (2023 −15%, 2024 +14%, 2025 +2%; FactSet). FY1 2026 is +7.3%, barely above the bar. Roughly half of revenue is recurring (service plus PDx, estimate). Equipment orders are lumpy and exposed to hospital budgets. Buyback support is small (~1.5 pts).
- P(no capex/cost shock year in 5y) ≈ 45%; P(each normal year ≥7%) ≈ 0.75 → 0.45 × 0.24 = 0.11
- Shock path: 0.55 × 0.25 × 0.75^4 ≈ 0.04
- Small allowance for margin-plan momentum → **P(Test 3) ≈ 18%**. FAIL.

## 6. Key uncertainty and what would change the view
The key question is whether GEHC can turn ~4–5% organic growth into ~10% EPS growth via margin expansion while absorbing input-cost and tariff headwinds. Evidence that would push Test 2 above 90%: delivering 16%+ adj EBIT margin by FY2027, mid-single-digit organic growth with China stabilised, and steady buybacks.

## 7. Earnings quality
**Borderline flag.** The adj vs GAAP gap is 11% 5y avg and 1% latest (FactSet), so it is fine. FCF conversion is 0.82x 10y and 0.81x 5y vs GAAP NI (FactSet), just above the 80% threshold. FY2025 pre-dividend FCF of $1.51bn is ~72% of net income of $2.08bn (FactSet, computed). The drivers are rising capex ($482m FY2025, FactSet), separation costs and working capital. Watch for a persistent sub-80% reading.
